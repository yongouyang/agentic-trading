# Annotation — `2026-09-10.{json,txt}` (Phase 4), added 2026-09-11

**This file does not replace the artifact.** `2026-09-10.json` / `.txt` are
left byte-for-byte as written, because a pre-registered result is append-only.
This note records what Phase 4b (`docs/phase-4b-plan.md`, Fork D) determined
*about* it.

## 1. The stored verdict is labelled differently now

The artifact stores `VERDICT US: h1_revised` and `VERDICT HK: h1_revised`.
Under the vocabulary added in Phase 4b, both are **`insufficient_evidence`**.

The reason is in the artifact's own numbers: each lane's `power floor` —
the smallest mean 20d IC it could have detected at t = 2 — is **0.0298 (US)** and
**0.0493 (HK)**, and both exceed the bar's own **0.02** magnitude requirement.
A test whose detection floor sits above the effect it is testing for cannot
falsify that effect. So the FAIL says *this window could not tell*, not *no edge*.

`h1_revised` is retained in the vocabulary for the case it describes: a FAIL on a
bar the lane **could** have detected (floor ≤ 0.02). Neither lane is that case.

The measured intervals, for the record:

| lane | mean 20d IC | 95 % CI (approx.) | readable as |
|---|---|---|---|
| US | +0.0145 | −0.016 … +0.045 | 0 and 0.03 both inside |
| HK | −0.0192 | −0.069 … +0.030 | 0 and even a negative edge inside |

## 2. "Gate 2 can never confirm" was withdrawn, then measured

The artifact cites the Phase-4 justification that passing Gate 2 is never a
confirmation because *portfolio alpha over ~4 years needs IR ≥ 0.985*. That IR
was **assumed** from a "realistic screen IR of 0.3–0.7"; the differential series
had no interval anywhere in the codebase. Phase 4b withdrew the claim as
unsupported and then measured it (D2, on the shifted window):

| lane | daily arithmetic mean | tracking error | IR | NW t (lag 20) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| US | +0.03 % | 15.85 %/yr | **0.49** | **1.19** |
| HK | −0.02 % | 9.28 %/yr | −0.59 | −1.20 |

So the conclusion survives on measurement — the realized US IR is 0.49 against
the 0.985 needed, squarely inside the range the original assumption guessed — but
that is a *measurement now*, not a guess then. Gate 2 remains falsification-only.

Note also that the artifact's headline differential (+38.03 %) and the D2 t-stat
are **different statistics**: the headline is a difference of compounded returns,
the t is on the mean of the daily arithmetic difference. They agree only to first
order.

## 3. The top-15 spread in this artifact is not the same statistic in both lanes

`spreadSeries` splits on a fixed `rank ≤ 15`. Against the artifact's own breadth
that is **8.3 % of US breadth but 60 % of HK's** — so the printed
`top-15-vs-rest spread` is a near-decile contrast in one lane and a
top-60 %-vs-rest contrast in the other, and the two numbers are not comparable.
Phase 4b re-reports this with a proportional cutoff (`max(ceil(0.10 × breadth), 5)`),
where HK's contrast is the top 5 of 25. Descriptive only; see the 09-11 artifact.

## 4. Reproducibility caveat

Re-running the runner does **not** reproduce this artifact's numbers exactly, and
the reason is structural rather than a defect: the store holds a rolling ~5-year
window refreshed by each `screen:daily`, so the replay's warmup boundary advances
with the run date. The 2026-09-11 run covers 2022-09-12…2026-09-11 — the same 1003
sessions, shifted two sessions later. US mean 20d IC moved +0.0145 → +0.0141 and
the equal-weight benchmark +50.92 % → +48.07 % for that reason. **This artifact
remains the reference for the pre-registered Phase-4 verdict**; the 09-11 numbers
are the calibration diagnostics.
