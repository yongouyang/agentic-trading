Quant Investing Fundamentals — Metrics, Strategy & Risk

Use data and models instead of subjective judgment to find patterns and opportunities in the market — evaluate strategies by risk-adjusted returns, and survive first. (用数据和模型代替主观判断，寻找市场中的规律和机会；活下来，才能赚到钱。)

1The Essence of Quantitative Investing (量化的本质)

The first step of quant investing: Understand → Metrics → Strategy → Risk (理解 → 指标 → 策略 → 风险).

"Quantitative investing replaces subjective judgment with data and models, seeking patterns and opportunities in the market."
(用数据和模型代替主观判断，寻找市场中的规律和机会。)
keyword 1

Data-driven (数据驱动)

Decisions come from market data and statistics, not gut feeling or tips.

keyword 2

Model (模型)

Explicit, testable rules — indicators, signals, position sizing — encode the investment logic.

keyword 3

Execution (执行)

Rules are executed with discipline and consistency, removing emotional trading.

★Quant investing is a marathon, not a sprint (量化投资是一场马拉松，不是短跑) — steady daily progress compounds into long-term results.

2Core Metrics (核心指标入门)

Get to know the most commonly used metrics first — together they measure a strategy's return ability and risk level.

Metric (指标)Meaning (含义)Purpose (作用)
Return (收益率)Profit level over a period of timeMeasures the strategy's money-making ability (衡量策略赚钱能力)
Max Drawdown (最大回撤, Max DD)Largest peak-to-trough declineMeasures the magnitude of risk (衡量风险大小)
Win Rate (胜率)Share of profitable trades among all tradesMeasures strategy stability (衡量策略稳定性)
Profit Factor (盈亏比)Total profits ÷ total losses (盈利总额 / 亏损总额)Measures the strategy's profit/loss structure (盈亏结构)
Annual Return (年化收益)Return expressed on an annualized basisMeasures long-run return level (衡量长期收益水平)
Sharpe Ratio (夏普比率)Excess return per unit of riskMeasures risk-adjusted return — >1 is good, >2 is excellent
💡 Remember: high return ≠ good strategy — the key is the risk-adjusted return (高收益不等于好策略，关键看风险调整后收益！).

How the metrics fit together

  • Return / Annual Return answer "how much did it make?"
  • Max Drawdown answers "how bad could it get along the way?"
  • Win Rate × Profit Factor together answer "is the P&L structure sustainable?" — a low win rate can still be profitable if the profit factor is high, and vice versa.
  • Sharpe Ratio compresses return and risk into one number for comparison across strategies.
★Never evaluate a strategy on return alone — always pair return metrics with risk and stability metrics.

3Strategy Logic Hands-On (策略逻辑体验)

A simple trend-following example: buy on the golden cross, sell on the death cross (均线金叉买入，均线死叉卖出).

exampleMA Crossover — a first trend-following strategy

Strategy steps (策略步骤):
  1. Compute the short-term MA (e.g. 5-day) and the long-term MA (e.g. 20-day).
  2. Short MA crosses above long MA → buy (金叉).
  3. Short MA crosses below long MA → sell (死叉).
  4. Set stop-loss / take-profit levels to control risk (设置止损止盈).
💡 Tip: every strategy has a market environment it suits — backtest validation is indispensable (任何策略都有适用的市场环境，回测验证必不可少！).
Core points (核心要点) ✓ Trends have continuity (趋势有连续性)
✓ Moving averages smooth out small fluctuations (均线、波动率小)
✓ Start from simple strategies — understanding the logic matters most (先从简单策略开始，理解逻辑最重要！)

How to interpret them (如何解读？)

  • Annual return 10.6% — the strategy is profitable, and the return level is acceptable.
  • Max drawdown 6% — the drawdown is within a controllable range; risk is relatively small.
  • Win rate 60% + profit factor 1.8 — decent win rate with profit factor > 1, so the overall profit expectation is positive (整体盈利期望为正).
  • Sharpe ratio 1.25 — risk-adjusted return is good (>1 is good, >2 is excellent).
⚠️ Caution (注意): Backtest results only represent historical performance — they do not guarantee future returns! They must be evaluated together with the market environment, capital management, and risk-control measures. (回测结果只能代表历史表现，不能保证未来收益！需要结合市场环境、资金管理和风控措施综合评估。)
★Key idea: don't look at returns alone — look at risk and stability even more (不要只看收益，更要看风险和稳定性！).

5Risk Control Is the Key (风险控制是关键)

Survive first, then profit (活下来，才能赚到钱) — the four pillars of risk control.

pillar 1

Control Max Drawdown (控制最大回撤)

Keep the max loss per trade or overall within what you can bear. Reference: generally ≤ 20%; aggressive strategies ≤ 30%.

pillar 2

Position Management (仓位管理)

Never go all-in; allocate capital sensibly (e.g. ≤ 10% per position). Core principle: leave room to handle uncertain market swings (留有余地，才能应对不确定的市场波动).

pillar 3

Stop-Loss Mechanism (止损机制)

Set stop-loss lines and execute them strictly — don't let small losses become big ones. Practices: fixed-percentage stop (e.g. 5%); technical stop (e.g. breaking a key level 跌破关键位).

pillar 4

Diversification (分散投资)

Don't put all eggs in one basket. Common ways: different industries / instruments; different strategy combinations; different markets (A-shares / US stocks / commodities, etc.).

"Manage risk well and returns follow naturally; protect your principal first — only then can you talk about returns."
(风险管理做好，收益自然水到渠成；先保住本金，才有机会谈收益！)

Risk-control checklist (风险控制清单)

✓ Before trading

  • I know the maximum loss I can bear (我知道自己最大能承受多少亏损).
  • I have set stop-losses and execute them strictly (我设置了止损，并且严格执行).
  • I control position sizes reasonably (我合理控制了仓位).

✓ Ongoing discipline

  • I diversify to reduce single-point risk (我分散了投资，降低单一风险).
  • I review regularly and refine the risk rules (我定期复盘，优化风控规则).
  • I don't chase overnight riches — I pursue long-term stability (我不追求一夜暴富，追求长期稳定).
★The core of steady profits is not predicting the market — it is managing risk (稳定盈利的核心，不是预测市场，而是管理风险！).

Study tips (学习小贴士)

  • Get hands-on first — don't stay at the theory level (先动手实践，不要只停留在理论).
  • Start with simple strategies, then add complexity gradually (从简单策略开始，逐步增加复杂度).
  • Record problems and insights promptly — build your own knowledge base (及时记录问题和心得，形成自己的知识库).
  • Stay patient — quant investing is a long-term accumulation process (保持耐心，量化投资是长期积累的过程).

Advice for beginners (给初学者的建议)

  • Start from simple strategies and advance step by step (从简单策略开始，逐步进阶).
  • First make sure you don't lose big, then pursue returns (先保证不亏大钱，再追求收益).
  • Learn more, practice more, review more (多学习、多实践、多复盘).
  • Keep patience and discipline; avoid emotional trading (保持耐心和纪律，避免情绪化交易).
  • Quant investing is a marathon, not a sprint (量化投资是一场马拉松，不是短跑).
