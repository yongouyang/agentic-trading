Trends & Moving Averages — Don't Predict, Follow

The core idea of quantitative trend strategies: don't predict the market — identify the trend that has already formed, and follow it. (不预测市场，而是跟随趋势。)

Source: knowledge-base/day_3 (IMG_6495–IMG_6502), "30天学习量化投资 · Day 3: 趋势与移动平均线" · Extracted 2026-08-30

1Trend: Don't Predict — Follow

A trend is price showing a sustained direction over a period of time: persistently up, persistently down, or sideways.

Core idea: don't predict the market — follow the trend!
(核心思想：不预测市场，而是跟随趋势！)  ·  "The trend is your friend — until it turns away." (趋势是朋友，直到它转身离开。)

Three common market states (三种常见的市场状态)

Uptrend

上升趋势

Price keeps making higher highs and higher lows, moving overall upward. (价格不断创新高和新低，整体向上运行。)

Downtrend

下降趋势

Price keeps making lower lows and lower highs, moving overall downward. (价格不断创新低和新低，整体向下运行。)

Range / Sideways

横盘震荡

Price oscillates back and forth within a band, with no clear direction. (价格在一定区间内来回波动，没有明显的方向。)

The goal of a quantitative trend strategy (量化趋势策略的目标)

  • Do not predict whether tomorrow's price will rise or fall (不去预测明天价格会上涨还是下跌);
  • Instead, identify whether a trend has already formed right now (识别当前是否已经形成趋势);
  • Once a trend has formed, go with it — until the trend ends (一旦趋势形成，顺势而为，直到趋势结束).
★A trend strategy trades on confirmation, not prediction: you give up calling the top and the bottom in exchange for riding the middle of a confirmed move.

2What Is a Moving Average (MA)?

MA is the average of the closing prices of the most recent N periods. (MA 是最近 N 个周期收盘价的平均值。)

MA(N) = Sum of the last N closing prices (最近 N 个收盘价之和)N

💡 Tip: the closing price (Close) is the "C" of the K-line / candlestick (OHLC). (收盘价 Close 是 K 线的 "C"。)

ExampleWorked example: computing MA5

Day 1Day 2Day 3Day 4Day 5
1011121314
MA5 = (10 + 11 + 12 + 13 + 14) / 5 = 60 / 5 = 12 MA5 = 12 means the average level of the last 5 closes is 12.
  • MA is not one day's price — it is the average price over a period of time.
  • MA smooths short-term fluctuations (平滑短期波动) and reflects the trend more clearly.
💡 Price is the more volatile line; MA5 is the smoother line. Price zig-zags around its moving average; the MA traces the underlying direction. (价格更波动，MA5 更平滑。)
✏️ Practice ①: compute MA5 of 10, 12, 13, 15, 20 → MA5 = (10+12+13+15+20) / 5 = 70 / 5 = 14. Mastering the definition of MA is the first step to understanding trend strategies.

3Why Is It Called a "Moving" Average?

Because the average moves forward with time, always computed over the most recent N closes.

Example (continued)One new bar arrives — the window slides one step right

Step 1 — the first 5 days:
1011121314 → MA5 = 60/5 = 12
Step 2 — day 6 arrives with close 15:
10 1112131415 → MA5 = 65/5 = 13
  • Every day the oldest data point drops out and the newest one enters (去掉最早的一个数据，加入最新的一个数据);
  • The window size is always N (here 5);
  • That is why it is a "moving" average — imagine a window of length 5 that slides one step to the right each day.
✏️ Practice ②: if day 7 closes at 16, the new window is 12, 13, 14, 15, 16 → MA5 = (12+13+14+15+16) / 5 = 70 / 5 = 14. Understanding "moving" is the key to actually using MA well. (理解"移动"，才能真正用好 MA。)

4Short MA vs Long MA (短均线 vs 长均线)

Different MA periods reflect price information over different time horizons.

Short MA — reflects short-term change (短均线：反映短期变化)

  • Small period — e.g. MA5, MA10;
  • More sensitive to price changes, reacts faster;
  • Captures short-term rises and falls sooner.
📈 MA5 hugs the price line: fast and sensitive, but easily disturbed by noise — like a sprint runner. (短均线像快跑的选手，反应快但容易被干扰。)

Long MA — reflects long-term trend (长均线：反映长期趋势)

  • Large period — e.g. MA20, MA60;
  • Less sensitive to price changes, reacts slower;
  • Better represents the long-term trend direction.
🐢 MA20 moves smoothly: slow but stable, representing the long-term direction — like a marathon jogger. (长均线像慢跑的选手，稳定但反应慢。)

The meaning and role of moving averages (均线的意义)

Filter noise

过滤噪音

An MA filters out part of the short-term random fluctuation (noise), making the trend clearer.

Trend following

趋势跟随

Helps judge whether the market is currently in an uptrend, downtrend, or sideways consolidation.

Simplify decisions

简化决策

Reduces complex price data to a single line, making it easy to define strategy rules.

Inherent lag

存在滞后

MA is an average of historical data — it lags. You cannot buy precisely at the bottom or sell precisely at the top.

Important reminder: MA is not a tool for predicting the future — it helps us identify "the trend that has already formed".
(均线不是预测未来的工具，而是帮助我们识别"当前已经形成的趋势"。)
★Short MAs reflect short-term change, long MAs reflect long-term trend; MAs filter noise and simplify judgment but lag. Using multiple periods together — fast and slow — is the foundation of trend strategies. (两者结合，效果更好。)

5The Dual Moving Average Strategy (双均线策略)

Use the relationship between a short MA and a long MA to judge the market trend and decide the action.

↗ MA5 > MA20 → Buy or hold (买入或持有)

The short MA runs above the long MA — the market is in an uptrend (上升趋势). Bullish alignment (多头市场): MA5 above MA20 → stay long.

↘ MA5 < MA20 → Sell or stay flat (卖出或空仓)

The short MA runs below the long MA — the market is in a downtrend (下降趋势). Bearish alignment (空头市场): MA5 below MA20 → exit or stay out.

💡 Tip: MA5 / MA20 is a common combination; you can choose different parameters to match your own trading horizon. (MA5、MA20 是常用组合，你也可以根据自己的交易周期选择不同的参数。)

★Core idea: let the trend tell us the direction — we only need to follow it. (让趋势告诉我们方向，我们只需要跟随趋势。)

6The Weakness: Whipsaw in Choppy Markets (震荡行情)

In a sideways market, the short and long MAs can cross frequently — causing repeated buy/sell churn that is hard to profit from.

Frequent crossings = repeated signals = hard to make money. (频繁交叉 = 反复信号 = 难以盈利)
When the market has no clear trend and oscillates within a range, the short MA repeatedly crosses the long MA, emitting mutually contradictory signals. This is Whipsaw (鞭打效应) — like being lashed by a whip, the strategy is whipped in and out of the market, exhausting both capital and nerves.

✗ Consequences of Whipsaw (结果)

  • Frequent trading — transaction costs pile up (频繁买卖，交易成本增加);
  • Faked out by false breakouts — signals get triggered by noise (容易被假突破骗出信号);
  • Repeated stop-outs — the account bleeds continuously (反复止损，账户持续亏损).

Why does it happen? (为什么会发生)

  • Price oscillates inside a range between an upper resistance (上方压力位) and a lower support (下方支撑位), with no clear direction;
  • The short MA is sensitive to price changes, so it easily crosses above and below the long MA;
  • Crossover signals appear in large numbers during chop — but most of them are false signals (假信号).

How to reduce Whipsaw (如何减少 Whipsaw 的影响 — covered in later days)

Trend filter

增加趋势过滤

Trade only when a trend is clear — e.g. consider buying only when price is above the long MA.

Widen the period gap

扩大均线周期差

E.g. use MA10 and MA60 instead — fewer, more meaningful crossings.

Combine indicators

结合其他指标

Confirm signals with trend strength (ADX), volume, etc. to improve reliability.

Stop-loss / take-profit

设置止损止盈

Cap the loss on each trade so small losses never grow into big ones.

Strategy performance by market regime (重点记住)

Market regime (行情类型)MA strategy performance (均线策略表现)
Trending — one-directional (趋势行情, 单边)Works well; signals are valid (效果较好，信号有效)✅
Choppy — sideways (震荡行情, 横盘)Prone to Whipsaw; poor results (容易 Whipsaw，效果较差)✗
★MA strategies perform well in trending markets but get whipsawed in ranges — they need extra filtering and risk control. Knowing a strategy's weakness is the first step to improving it. (认识弱点，才能更好地改进策略。)

7Exercises & Day 3 Summary

Consolidate the MA calculation and see how trend strategies behave across different market environments.

Worked exercises (练习题解析)

① Rising prices — 10, 12, 13, 15, 20: 1012131520 → MA5 = 70/5 = 14
② Next day closes at 25 — window slides to 12, 13, 15, 20, 25: 10 1213152025 → MA5 = 85/5 = 17
③ Perfectly flat — every close is 10: 1010101010 → MA5 = 50/5 = 10

Key conclusions (关键结论)

  • MA reflects the trend (MA 能反映趋势) — when price rises, MA rises step by step; when price is flat, MA stays flat; when price falls, MA falls.
  • MA lags (均线存在滞后) — it is an "after-the-fact indicator" (事后指标): you cannot buy precisely at the lowest point, nor sell at the highest.
  • Trend strategies have limits (趋势策略的局限) — in a purely sideways market with no clear trend, trend strategies usually struggle to profit.
