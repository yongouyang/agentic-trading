Order, Trade, Fees & Slippage — The Execution Chain

From "the strategy wants to buy" to "the account actually got filled" — there are many steps in between. Understanding these costs and constraints is the key to getting closer to real trading. (从"策略想买"到"账户真的成交"，中间还有很多环节。)

Source: knowledge-base/day_8 (IMG_6527–IMG_6542), "30天学习量化投资 · Day 8: 订单、成交、手续费与滑点" · Extracted 2025-08-30

1Order vs Trade — Intent vs Reality

Many beginners think "placing an order = getting filled". In reality, there is a world of uncertainty in between. (很多新手以为下单就等于成交，其实中间隔着很多不确定性。)

Order (订单) — the intent

  • Intent to trade (下单意图)
  • Tells the system: "I want to buy/sell X" (告诉系统：我想买/卖什么)
  • Contains: price, quantity, direction, type (包含：价格、数量、方向、类型等信息)
  • Result is uncertain (结果是不确定的)

Trade (成交) — the result

  • Actual fill result (实际成交结果)
  • The portion the market actually matched (市场真正撮合成功的部分)
  • Contains: fill price, quantity, time, fees, etc. (包含：成交价格、数量、时间、手续费等)
  • Result is certain (结果是确定的)
Key insight (关键认知): An Order only expresses "what I want to do" (想做什么). A Trade is what actually happened (真正发生的交易). Only a Trade changes the account (直接影响账户资金和持仓).

Stage-by-stage breakdown (逐环节解析)

StageWhat happensExampleKey property
① Signal Strategy generates a trade idea from market data and indicators (策略根据行情和指标，产生交易信号) Price breaks above MA → produce "buy" signal Just an "idea" — no funds or positions involved (信号只是"想法"，不涉及资金和持仓)
② Order Convert signal into a concrete order instruction (将信号转化为具体的下单指令) Buy 2000 shares, limit 10.00, direction: buy A request to the market, not yet filled (Order 还没有成交，只是向市场发出的请求)
③ Trade Market matching produces the actual fill (市场撮合后，得到实际成交的结果) Filled 1500 shares @ 10.05 Fill price, quantity, time may all differ from order (成交价格、数量、时间等都可能与订单不同)
④ Portfolio Update positions and cash based on fill result (根据成交结果，更新持仓和资金) Position +1500, cash reduced accordingly The only source of account state change (账户状态变化的唯一来源)
⑤ Equity Recompute total equity from latest positions and prices (根据最新持仓和市场价格，计算总权益) Equity = Cash + all position market value Ultimate measure of whether the account is making or losing money (最终衡量账户赚还是亏的指标)
💡 Core insight (核心认知): A signal produces an "idea", an order expresses an "intent", and a fill produces a "result". Only the fill changes the account state, which then affects total equity. Quant trading is essentially about standardizing, controlling, and backtesting this chain. (量化交易的本质，就是把这个链路标准化、可控化、可回测化。)
★See opportunity → Place order → Get filled → Update positions → Impact equity — this is the complete trading chain! (看到机会 → 下单 → 成交 → 更新持仓 → 影响权益，这就是完整的交易链路！)

3Slippage — The Price You See ≠ The Price You Get

The price you see is not necessarily the price you get. The difference between the actual fill price and the expected price is called slippage. (看到的价格，不一定能以这个价格成交，实际成交价偏离预期价格的部分，就是滑点。)

costWhat is slippage? (什么是滑点？)

Slippage (滑点)= Actual fill price (实际成交价)− Expected price (看到的价格)

Example: slippage on a buy (买入时的滑点)

Expected (预期)

You see the price at10.00
You want to buy at10.00

Actual (实际)

Fill price10.05
Slippage+0.05 (5 basis points)

Slippage directly increases trading costs and reduces final returns. If backtests ignore slippage, returns are typically overestimated. (滑点会直接增加交易成本，降低最终收益。回测时如果忽略滑点，收益通常会被高估。)

Common sources of slippage (滑点的常见来源)

Market volatility (市场波动)

Prices change rapidly; by the time you order, the price is different.

Low liquidity (流动性不足)

Few orders on the book — your order must take worse prices.

Large order size (订单规模大)

Big orders eat through multiple price levels (冲击成本, market impact).

Network / system latency (网络与系统延迟)

Time between signal and order — market has already moved.

Matching rules (撮合机制)

Exchange priority rules also affect fill prices.

Impact on returns (滑点对收益的影响)

Ideal (no slippage)

Buy price10.00
Sell price10.50
Profit0.50 (5%)

Real (with slippage)

Buy price10.05 (+0.05)
Sell price10.45 (−0.05)
Profit0.40 (4%)
💡 Slippage makes you: ① buy at a higher price, ② sell at a lower price, ③ earn less than expected. Slippage is part of trading costs and must be accounted for in both backtests and live trading! (滑点是交易成本的一部分，必须在回测和实盘中认真考虑！)

4Commission Fees — The Cost of Every Trade

Every fill incurs a fee — this is an unavoidable cost in real trading. (每一笔成交都会产生手续费，这是真实交易中必不可少的成本。)

Fee (手续费)= Trade amount (成交金额)× Commission rate (手续费率)

brokerFee calculation (手续费计算)

Common commission rates (常见手续费率):
MarketRateNote
A-shares (A股)~0.03% (万三)Varies by broker; may differ across markets
US stocks$0 or per-shareMany zero-commission brokers, but PFOF exists
Crypto0.05%–0.1%Maker/taker rates differ
Quick example (简单计算) Trade amount: 100,000 yuan
Commission rate: 0.03% (万三)
Fee = 100,000 × 0.0003 = 30 yuan

Impact on the account (手续费对账户的影响)

ActionTrade amountFeeReal cost / returnAccount change
Buy or sellYour spend= amount × rateBuy cost increases; sell return decreasesAccount decreases accordingly

Don't ignore the small numbers (别忽略小数点后三位)

Accumulation example Buy 100,000 yuan of stock → fee = 30 yuan per trade

If you trade 100 times per year: 30 × 100 = 3,000 yuan in fees annually.
Frequent trading significantly erodes returns — controlling trade frequency is important! (频繁交易会大幅侵蚀收益，控制交易频率很重要！)
💡 Day 8 key point: Although each fee is tiny, they accumulate significantly over time. Quantitative systems must include fees! (手续费虽然每次很小，但长期累计会显著影响收益，做量化一定要把手续费算进去！)
Tip: Beyond commissions, there may be stamp duty, transfer fees, and exchange fees to consider in backtests and live trading.

5Partial Fill — Not All Orders Get Fully Filled

Market liquidity is finite. Your order may not be completely filled. (市场的流动性有限，你下的订单可能无法全部成交。)

Full fill — ideal (完整成交)

  • Order quantity = 2,000 shares
  • Filled quantity = 2,000 shares ✓

Partial fill — reality (部分成交)

  • Order quantity = 2,000 shares
  • Filled quantity = 1,200 shares ✗

brokerWhy partial fills happen (为什么会部分成交)

Insufficient depth (市场深度不足)

Not enough buy/sell orders available at your price level.

Large order impact (大单冲击)

Big orders are split into multiple fills across price levels.

Fast price movement (价格快速变化)

Remaining quantity can't be filled as price moves away.

Aggressive limit price (限价太激进)

Only part of the order meets the price condition.

Core constraint (核心约束)

Trade.Quantity ≤ Order.Quantity — actual fill quantity is always less than or equal to order quantity.

Market depth example (市场买盘深度示例)

Price (元)Available qty (可卖数量)Fill result
10.00800Filled 800 shares
10.01500Filled 400 shares (remaining from 1,200 target)
10.02300Not filled — order exhausted
Partial fill processing example Order: buy 2,000 shares @ limit 10.00
Actual fill: 1,200 shares @ average 10.005

System handling:
① Generate Order for 2,000 shares
② Market matches 1,200 shares only
③ Update Position: +1,200 shares
④ Update Portfolio: cash reduced by 1,200 × 10.005 + fees
⑤ Remaining 800 shares: unfilled, awaiting further action or cancellation
💡 In real markets, partial fills are the norm, not the exception! A good trading system must correctly handle every "unfilled" remainder. (在真实市场中，部分成交是常态，不是例外！好的交易系统，必须正确处理每一次"未成交"。)

7Why Real Returns Fall Short of Ideal Backtests

Backtests assume ideal conditions. Real trading is full of "hidden costs" that continuously erode returns. (回测往往假设理想条件，真实交易中存在多种"隐形成本"，让收益被不断侵蚀。)

The five hidden costs (五大隐形成本)

①

Fees (手续费)

Every trade incurs costs. Frequent trading → high cumulative cost.

②

Slippage (滑点)

The price you see ≠ the price you get.

③

Latency (延迟)

Time gap between signal and fill; market has already moved.

④

Partial fills (部分成交)

Order qty > filled qty; can't trade at the expected price/size.

⑤

Market impact (冲击成本)

Large orders push the market price against you.

The essence of quant trading is seeking a probabilistic edge amid uncertainty. (策略的理论收益，不等于真实交易收益。只有把成本、滑点、延迟、成交情况考虑进来，才能更接近真实世界。量化交易的本质，是在不确定性中，争取概率上的优势。)

Remember: backtests tell you "what you could earn"; trading tells you "what you actually earn." Reducing friction is improving win rate! (回测告诉你"可能赚多少"，交易告诉你"最终赚多少"。减少损耗，提升胜率，才是长期稳定盈利的关键！)
