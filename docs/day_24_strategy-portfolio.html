Strategy Portfolio — Diversification & Risk Reduction

Don't put all your money into one strategy — a single strategy may fail, but a good portfolio can survive across different market environments. (不要把所有钱押在一个策略上。) The core goal of portfolio investing: reduce risk, not increase returns.

1Strategy vs Portfolio — Completely Different Roles

Strategy finds opportunities ("what to buy"). Portfolio allocates capital ("how much to give"). They are both essential but serve different purposes.

Strategy (策略)

Q: What trading opportunities exist?

  • Discovers tradeable opportunities in the market
  • Focus: entry/exit signals, stop-loss logic, expected returns
  • "I found a long signal — I expect it to rise!"

Portfolio (组合)

Q: How much capital for this opportunity?

  • Decides how much capital each strategy uses
  • Focus: capital allocation, risk exposure, correlation, overall volatility
  • "Nice signal — but I'll only give you 30% of capital!"
Key distinction: Strategy gives you the opportunity to earn. Portfolio determines how much you actually earn or lose. (Strategy 让你有机会赚钱，Portfolio 决定你最终赚多少钱、亏多少！)

2Why Strategy Combinations? — No Single Strategy Works Everywhere

Markets cycle through uptrends, sideways, and downtrends. No single strategy works well in all phases.

3Capital Allocation — Weights Determine Risk Exposure

Assets aren't about concentrated bets — they're about distributing capital across strategies.

A: 90%, B: 5%, C: 5% — if A fails, the account suffers massive swings.

💡 Weights can be dynamically adjusted. Periodically evaluate and rebalance based on market conditions and strategy performance — directing capital toward stronger strategies. (权重可以动态调整，让资金始终流向更有优势的策略。)

4Correlation — The Heart of Diversification

The portfolio's real focus isn't return level — it's correlation. Low-correlation good strategies beat high-correlation great strategies.

conceptWhat is correlation?

High correlation (≈ +1)

Two strategies' returns rise and fall together. If one is up, the other is up too. Same direction, same time.

Low correlation (≈ 0 or negative)

Strategies' returns are out of sync. When one loses, the other might win. Different rhythms.

Correlation scale

−1: Perfect negative
0: Uncorrelated
+1: Perfect positive

💡 Higher correlation = worse diversification. Lower correlation = better volatility reduction. When building portfolios, prioritize low-correlation or negatively-correlated strategies. (相关性越高，组合分散效果越差；相关性越低，组合降低波动的效果越好。)

5Why Low Correlation Reduces Volatility — Profit/Loss Offset

When two strategies move differently, one's loss is often offset by the other's gain — making the combined curve much smoother.

High correlation (fall together)

Two curves nearly overlap. Earn together, lose together. Cannot reduce volatility.

Low correlation (offset each other)

Different rhythms. Profits and losses cancel out. Combined curve is much smoother!

The beauty of portfolio investing is not that every strategy earns a lot — but that different strategies' profits and losses can offset each other, making the overall curve smoother and longer-lasting. (组合投资的魅力，不在于每个策略都赚很多，而在于不同策略之间的盈亏能够互相抵消。)

6More Strategies ≠ Better Diversification

True diversification comes from different return sources, not strategy count. 10 highly correlated trend strategies ≈ 1 strategy.

trapThe false diversification trap

Example: 10 highly similar trend strategies MA5/20, MA6/20, MA5/21, MA6/21, MA7/22, … — all are trend-following. They're all betting that "trends will continue." When the market enters sideways or reversal mode, all 10 lose together!

True diversification: different return sources

Trend strategies

Earn from trends. Excel in uptrends.

Mean-reversion

Earn from oscillation. Excel in sideways.

Arbitrage

Earn from price spreads / convergence.

Event driven

Earn from event-driven shocks.

Macro hedging

Hedge systemic risk.

Different return sources take turns working in different market environments — this is the key to portfolio power.

Many strategies, high correlation

10 strategies that rise and fall together → essentially "one strategy". Cannot reduce volatility.

Moderate count, low correlation

5 strategies with distinct rhythms → true diversification. Combined curve much smoother.

💡 Quantity ≠ diversification. Low correlation + different return sources = true diversification. The portfolio's goal is not to pile on more strategies — it's to have uncorrelated strategies help each other. (数量多 ≠ 分散好。相关性低 + 收益来源不同 = 真正的分散。)

★Day 24 Summary — Portfolio's #1 Goal: Reduce Risk

Six key points

  1. Portfolio goal is risk reduction, not simply boosting returns — reduce volatility and drawdowns to survive long-term.
  2. Low-correlation combinations reduce volatility — different strategies' P&L offset in different environments.
  3. True diversification = different return sources, not strategy count. 10 correlated strategies ≈ 1 strategy.
  4. Portfolio solves "how much to allocate" — weights determine each strategy's risk exposure and contribution.
  5. Dynamic weight adjustment — rebalance periodically based on market changes and strategy performance.
  6. When one strategy fails, the portfolio stays stable — no dependency on a single strategy, able to survive bull and bear cycles.
