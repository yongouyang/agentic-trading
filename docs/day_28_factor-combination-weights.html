Factor Combination & Weights — Stable Regions, Not Historical Peaks

Multiple factors all work, but they contribute differently to future returns — each must be given a reasonable "voice" (话语权) via weights to build a stronger, more stable strategy. The goal is not the weight with the highest historical return, but a weight region that stays effective across different times and environments.

Source: knowledge-base/day_28 (IMG_6705–IMG_6712), "30天学习量化投资 · Day 28: 因子组合与权重" · Extracted 2026-09-02

1What Are Factor Weights? — The Composite Score

Multi-factor combination = each factor's score × weight → composite score (综合得分). The weight decides how much "voice" (话语权) each factor has in the portfolio.

A simple multi-factor weight example

Suppose we use 3 factors — Value (价值), Quality (质量), Momentum (动量) — and set weights that sum to 100%:

Value: 40%
Quality: 40%
Momentum: 20%

How to compute the composite score (example)

A stock's three factor scores (standardized to 0~100): Composite = Σ (factor score × weight)

Different weights → different composite scores → possibly different rankings → different final portfolio! (权重不同 → 综合得分不同 → 股票排名可能不同 → 最终投资组合也会不同！)

Weights reflect your investment logic and beliefs (投资逻辑和信念)

Different weights = different views

  • Value matters more → give Value a higher weight
  • Quality is more stable → give Quality a higher weight
  • Momentum is stronger → give Momentum a higher weight

Weights are not decided on a whim — they're based on:

  • ✅ Historical backtest results (历史回测效果)
  • ✅ Factor stability (因子稳定性)
  • ✅ Economic logic & market environment (经济逻辑与市场环境)
  • ✅ Risk-control considerations (风险控制考虑)
★Factor weights determine each factor's influence (影响力) in the portfolio — they directly affect the composite score, ranking, and final return performance. There is no absolute "correct answer" for weights — only a more suitable choice under specific assumptions and market environments. (权重没有绝对的"正确答案"，只有在特定假设和市场环境下更合适的选择！)

What we usually want: a region with high return + high Sharpe + low drawdown + high stability — not an isolated "spike point" (尖峰点). (通常我们希望：高收益 + 高夏普 + 低回撤 + 高稳定性 的区域，而不是某个孤立的"尖峰点")

Grid search caveats (注意事项)

Reasonable step size

Too large → coarse, may miss good regions; too small → huge compute, limited gain. Common: 5% or 10%. (步长选择要合理)

Avoid unreasonable combos

Cap single-factor weights (e.g. ≤ 70%) or set a minimum threshold (e.g. ≥ 10%) to keep combos stable. (避免不合理组合，让组合更稳定)

Rolling backtest validation

Full-window results can mislead; use rolling windows to check performance across periods. Look at the long run, not one stretch. (使用滚动回测验证)

Filter with economic logic

Backtest is a tool, not the whole story. Final choices must fit assumptions, logic, and practical executability. Logic + data = good strategy. (结合经济逻辑筛选)

★Grid search is not for finding the historically "best" weight — it's for discovering weight regions with stable performance and strong robustness (鲁棒性强)! Next: after finding a good region, how to further verify stability? (找到好的区域后，如何进一步验证稳定性？)

4The Historical-Optimum Trap — The Peak May Be Overfitting

The point with the highest backtest return is often luck (运气), not skill (能力). The "optimal weight" found in historical data may not keep working in the future! (历史最优权重陷阱：最高点可能只是过拟合)

Why does overfitting happen? (为什么会过拟合？)

Search space too large

Hundreds or thousands of weight combos tested — some point will "look" best by chance. (搜索空间过大)

Historical data contains noise

Noise in the backtest may be amplified by a particular weight combo, producing a false edge (虚假优势). (历史数据包含噪声)

Market changes not considered

Future environments, factor effectiveness, correlations all change; historical optimality can't guarantee the future. (未考虑市场变化)

Lacking constraints & logic

Weights without economic logic are a "numbers game" (数字游戏), with no stable theoretical support. (缺乏约束和逻辑)

📌 Remember: overfitting isn't because the model is bad — it's because we used the wrong method to find the "best answer". What we really want is not a point, but a stable region! (过拟合不是模型太差，而是我们用错了方法去寻找"最佳答案"。真正要找的不是点，而是稳定区域！)
★The historically optimal weight may just be luck; a stable, effective region is closer to skill! (历史最优的权重，可能只是运气；稳定有效的区域，才更接近能力！)

5Stable Regions — Look for a Plateau, Not a Spike

What we want is not the point with the highest historical return, but a region where everything nearby also performs well. (稳定区域：寻找平台，而不是尖峰)

Spike vs plateau: two different outcomes

Case A: historical optimum (尖峰 spike)

✗ Only one point is especially high; the moment you move slightly around it, returns fall sharply. (只有一个点特别高，周围稍微变动收益就大幅下降。)

Case B: stable plateau (稳定平台)

✓ A whole area performs well; insensitive to small weight changes — more stable! (一大片区域表现都不错，对权重的微小变化不敏感，更稳定！)

Benefits of a stable region (稳定区域的好处)

More robust (更稳健)

Small changes don't cause big return swings; the future is more reliable (未来更可靠).

More overfit-resistant (更抗过拟合)

Doesn't depend on one specific number; reduces the influence of historical noise (降低历史噪声的影响).

Easier to execute (更易执行)

No need for precision to several decimal places; lower maintenance cost (降低维护成本).

More extensible (更有延展性)

When adding new factors later, easier to find suitable combos within the region.

How to define a "stable region"?

Reference multiple metrics at once

  • ☑ Annualized return (年化收益) ↑
  • ☑ Max drawdown (最大回撤) — the smaller the better ↓
  • ☑ Sharpe — the bigger the better ↑
  • ☑ IC mean (IC 均值) — the bigger the better ↑
  • ☑ Win rate / profit-loss ratio (胜率 / 盈亏比)

Definition rules (example)

  • ☑ Annualized ≥ 80% of the best value
  • ☑ Sharpe ≥ 80% of the best value
  • ☑ Max drawdown ≤ 120% of the best value
  • ☑ IC mean positive and stable (IC 均值为正且稳定)

Combinations meeting the conditions = the stable region (满足条件的组合 = 稳定区域). On the ternary diagram: green dots = meet conditions (stable region), blue dots = fail conditions — they form a contiguous "plateau", not a single spike.

★When choosing weight combinations, don't fixate on the highest-return point — look for a plateau region with stable performance and forgiving boundaries (边界宽容)! Next: how to finalize the weight? A scientific and practical decision process. (如何确定最终权重？一个科学又实用的决策流程！)

6Out-of-Sample Validation — Do Weights Still Work in the Future?

The point of backtesting is not to prove "the past was great", but to verify "it may work in the future" (验证"未来可能有效"). If a weight combo only works historically, it's luck, not real skill.

What is in-sample vs out-of-sample? (样本内 vs 样本外)

In-sample / training period 样本内 (2018.01 – 2024.12)

During this time: we search for the optimal weight combination (e.g. 37%/48%/15%).

Out-of-sample / test period 样本外 (2024.12 – 2025.12)

During this time: we no longer adjust weights — we only observe real performance.

Core idea: if a weight combo is excellent in-sample but degrades badly out-of-sample, it very likely overfit history. Only combos that stay stable out-of-sample deserve trust! (如果一个权重组合在样本内非常优秀，但在样本外大幅变差，说明它很可能过拟合了历史。只有样本外依然表现稳定，才值得信任！)

Common pitfalls of OOS validation (常见陷阱)

Test period too short

Short windows can be skewed by chance events; recommend at least 1 year+. (测试期太短)

Market environment changed

If market style shifts dramatically, old factors may temporarily fail. (市场环境变化)

Frequent weight adjustments

Tweaking weights on every return dip makes future validation impossible. (频繁调整权重)

Returns only, no risk

Even OOS, still watch whether drawdown, volatility and other risk metrics are controlled. (只看收益不看风险)

The golden rules of OOS validation (黄金法则)

Rule 1: Independent data 独立数据

Test data must be fully independent from training data — no information leakage (不能有任何信息泄漏).

Rule 2: Stability first 稳定优先

OOS return doesn't need to be the highest; stable, sustainable, risk-controlled combos are good combos.

Rule 3: Multi-dimensional 多维评价

Judge with return, drawdown, Sharpe, win rate, IC stability and more together.

Rule 4: Long-term tracking 长期跟踪

One successful test isn't the end — keep tracking real performance and review periodically (定期复盘).

★Only weight combinations that pass out-of-sample validation (样本外验证) are qualified to go live (进入实盘)!

★Day 28 Summary — Simple, Stable, Explainable Beats Historical-Optimal

Weight optimization of a multi-factor combination is not about finding the number that made the most money historically — it's about finding a weight region with economic logic (经济逻辑), stable nearby performance, and continued out-of-sample effectiveness. 最优可能是运气，稳定才更接近能力！

Core knowledge learned today

  1. Factor combinations need weights — multiple factors weighted into a composite score (按权重加权，形成综合得分).
  2. Don't blindly chase the historical optimum — overfitting historical noise may fail in the future (不要盲目追求历史最优).
  3. Look for stable effective regions — choose weights inside an area that all performs well (寻找稳定有效区域).
  4. Combine with economic logic — roughly reasonable, explainable weights are more trustworthy (权重大致合理，可解释，才更可信).
  5. Out-of-sample validation is key — only what still works OOS deserves trust (样本外验证是关键).

Three typical outcomes of weight selection

Historical optimum (尖峰)

  • Highest historical return, highest Sharpe
  • But small nearby moves → returns drop sharply
  • Very likely fails in the future

✗ Risk: overfitting (过拟合)

Stable plateau (推荐 recommended)

  • A whole region performs well
  • Nearby changes have small impact
  • Still effective out-of-sample — more stable, more reliable

✅ Preferred choice (优先选择)

Low-return region (低洼)

  • Overall low returns
  • No amount of tweaking lifts it
  • More time invested, limited improvement

✗ Not prioritized (不优先考虑)

How to land the final weight? (最终权重如何落地？)

  • ☑ Weights don't need to be complex: keep it simple (e.g. 3–5 factors)
  • ☑ Weights don't need to be precise: e.g. 35% / 45% / 20% is fine
  • ☑ Periodic review & optimization: check every half-year or year (定期复盘优化)
  • ☑ Control trading costs: frequent weight changes increase costs (控制交易成本)
  • ☑ Combine with risk control: even great factors need drawdown control (与风险控制结合)

Common misconceptions vs correct understanding (常见误区 vs 正确认知)

✗ Common misconceptions

  • Chasing the highest historical-return weight
  • Weights precise to two decimal places
  • Frequently adjusting weights, chasing rallies and dumping dips (追涨杀跌)
  • Ignoring economic logic and portfolio explanation
  • Skipping out-of-sample validation

✓ Correct understanding

  • Look for stable effective regions
  • Simple, rough weights are fine (权重简单、粗略即可)
  • Weights relatively stable, long-term effective
  • The combination needs economic-logic support
  • Out-of-sample validation is a must (必选项)
★What we seek is not the historically "most profitable" weight, but a weight combination more worthy of trust in the future — one that can accompany us for the long term! (我们寻找的不是历史上"最赚钱"的权重，而是未来更值得信任、能够长期陪伴的权重组合！)
