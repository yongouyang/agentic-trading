From Hypothesis to Strategy — The Scientific Method

Quantitative investing isn't about finding "historical answers" — it's about proposing a hypothesis, then using data to validate it. (量化投资不是找"历史答案"，而是提出假设，再用数据验证它！)

✓ Correct flow: hypothesis → data

  • Propose an interpretable market hypothesis
  • Translate into quantifiable rules (indicators, conditions, entry/exit)
  • Backtest on history → out-of-sample validation → explain why → cautious launch

✗ Wrong flow: parameters →上线

  • Treat history as an "answer-finding tool" — endlessly tune parameters
  • Looks great on history → think you found the holy grail
  • Skip validation → go live → likely overfitting → fails in new markets
Core insight: Quantitative investing is not predicting the future — it's building long-term, repeatable advantages through verifiable hypotheses. (量化投资的核心，不是预测未来，而是通过可验证的假设，构建长期可重复的优势。)

1Market Hypothesis — The Starting Point Is Your Understanding

The strategy starts with your cognition about the market, not with data or parameters. (策略的起点不是数据，而是你的认知！)

hypothesisWhat is a market hypothesis?

A market hypothesis (市场假设) is your belief or conjecture about how the market operates. It determines your strategy's direction and design logic.

Common market hypotheses

Trend continuity (趋势具有连续性)

After a price rise, it's more likely to continue rising in the short term.

Mean reversion (均值具有回归性)

Prices that deviate from the mean tend to return to it.

Volatility clustering (波动率具有聚集性)

High volatility periods tend to be followed by more high volatility.

Volume-price sync (量价具有同步性)

Volume increases confirm price trends as more reliable.

Event impact (事件会带来影响)

Earnings reports, policy changes cause price fluctuations.

What makes a good hypothesis?

CriterionDescription
Logical (有逻辑)Can be explained by fundamentals, market behavior, or experience
Verifiable (可验证)Can be tested through historical data
Stable (稳定存在)Likely effective across different times and markets
Tradable (有交易意义)Generates positive expectancy when the conditions hold
💡 A strategy without a hypothesis is essentially "blindly searching for answers in historical data". The foundation of quant strategies is an interpretable market hypothesis, not the best parameters cherry-picked from history. (没有假设的策略，本质上是在历史数据里"瞎找答案"。量化策略的根基，是可解释的市场假设。)

Bad vs good hypotheses

✗ Bad (parameter conclusions, not hypotheses)

  • "MA5=7 is best" — this is a parameter conclusion
  • "This indicator historically made the most money" — this is a result, not a reason

✓ Good (interpretable market beliefs)

  • "Trends have continuity" — testable, logical
  • "When semiconductor ETF is in an uptrend, it's more likely to continue"

2Translating Hypothesis into Executable Rules

The hypothesis is the "idea"; the rules are "executable conditions" that a computer can act on. (假设是"想法"，规则是"可执行的条件"！)

exampleSemiconductor ETF Trend Strategy (半导体ETF趋势策略)

Hypothesis: Trends have continuity (趋势具有连续性)

Indicators chosen:
TrendMoving averages (MA5, MA20)
Volatility20-day volatility
Buy rules (all must be true):
  • MA5 > MA20 (uptrend confirmed)
  • 20-day volatility < 20% (low-volatility environment)
Sell rules (any one triggers):
  • MA5 < MA20 (trend broken)
  • 20-day volatility > 20% (too volatile)
  • Stop-loss triggered (e.g., −8% from buy price)

Rule design principles

  • Clear (明确) — conditions must be calculable and verifiable
  • Simple (简洁) — too many rules → overfitting; start simple
  • Executable (可执行) — must be judgeable with real-time data in the future
  • Adjustable but limited (可调整但有限度) — parameters have meaning; don't tune for max historical return
💡 Hypothesis tells us "why we do it"; rules tell the computer "how to do it". (假设告诉我们"为什么做"，规则告诉计算机"怎么做"。)

3Validating the Strategy — More Than Just Returns

Backtesting profitability is just the first checkpoint. The goal: determine if the hypothesis + rules will likely still work in the future.

validationBacktest metrics (回测指标)

Return metrics

Cumulative return, annualized return

Risk metrics

Max drawdown, drawdown duration

Quality metrics

Sharpe ratio, win rate, P/L ratio

Trade metrics

Trade count, avg holding period, frequency

Cost factors

Fees, slippage, market impact — many strategies profitable before costs are unprofitable after!

Key validation dimensions (关键验证维度)

DimensionQuestionMethod
Out-of-sample (样本外)Does it work on unseen data?Train on 2015–2021, test on 2022–2024
Cross-year (不同年份)Bull, bear, sideways markets?Test in 2018, 2020, 2021, 2022 separately
Cross-instrument (不同ETF)Works on other similar instruments?Test on 512480, 159995, 513330
Parameter stabilitySmall parameter changes → stable results?MA short: 5→3–8; MA long: 20→15–30
Cost sensitivityStill profitable with higher costs?Fees: 0.05%→0.20%; slippage: 0.02%→0.10%
Remember: Backtesting only proves the strategy worked in the past. Validation's purpose is to judge whether it will probably work in the future. Only strategies that pass all layers of validation deserve live capital. (回测只是"证明策略过去有效"，验证的目的是"判断策略未来是否大概率有效"。)

4Interpretability — Know Why You're Making Money

Not knowing why you're making money is like driving in a black box — you can't see the road or the bottom. (不知道为什么赚钱，就像在黑箱里开车。)

whyWhy interpretability is critical

Losses are normal in quant trading — consecutive losses too. You need to judge: is this a normal drawdown, or has the strategy stopped working?

Interpretable strategy (可解释的策略)

  • I know why it makes money
  • I understand what conditions it works under
  • I know where it might fail
  • During losses: I can judge normal drawdown vs strategy failure
  • Result: confidence, evidence, long-term persistence

Black-box strategy (黑箱策略)

  • I only know it historically made money
  • I don't know why it works
  • I don't know what conditions make it fail
  • During losses: impossible to judge — only guesswork
  • Result: no confidence, reliance on luck, hard to survive

When is interpretability most important?

During consecutive losses

Judge: normal drawdown or strategy failure? Continue / adjust / stop?

When market regime changes

Is the current environment still within the strategy's effective range?

During parameter tuning

Know each parameter's meaning — don't just optimize for historical performance.

When communicating to others

Clear explanations earn trust and enable feedback.

Self-check: 5 questions before going live

1
Why does this strategy make money? (What's the underlying logic?)
2
Under what conditions is it effective? (Market, volatility, cycle, etc.)
3
Under what conditions would it fail? (What are the risk scenarios?)
4
Have I validated with out-of-sample data?
5
If consecutive losses occur, how do I decide whether to continue?
💡 "Don't trade strategies you can't explain." Being able to explain doesn't guarantee profits — but not being able to explain guarantees eventual losses. (能解释 ≠ 一定能赚钱，但不能解释 = 迟早会亏钱！)
