Candlesticks & OHLCV Data (K线与 OHLCV 数据)

Understand the most fundamental data structure behind every quant strategy: one candlestick = one time period, five core numbers — Open, High, Low, Close, Volume. (看懂量化策略最基础的数据结构。)

Source: knowledge-base/day_2 (IMG_6487–IMG_6494), "30天学习量化投资 · Day 2: K线与OHLCV数据" · Extracted 2026-08-30

1What Is OHLCV? (什么是 OHLCV)

One candlestick (K线) represents the price movement within a single time period. Every bar corresponds to five core data points.

O

Open 开盘价

The price at the start of the period (周期开始时的价格).

H

High 最高价

The highest price reached during the period (周期内达到的最高价格).

L

Low 最低价

The lowest price reached during the period (周期内达到的最低价格).

C

Close 收盘价

The price at the end of the period (周期结束时的价格).

V

Volume 成交量

The number of units traded during the period (周期内的成交数量).

Every candlestick corresponds to five core values: O, H, L, C, V.
OHLCV is the most basic and most commonly used data structure in quantitative investing. (OHLCV 是量化投资中最基础、最常用的数据结构。)
★Different periods (1-minute, 5-minute, daily, weekly, …) produce different K-line data. A candlestick is not just a picture — it is a row of structured data (更是一条结构化的数据).

2Bullish vs Bearish Candles (阳线与阴线)

The candle's color only expresses the relationship between close and open — it says nothing about the path taken during the day.

Bullish candle 阳线

Close > Open · 收盘价高于开盘价
High 最高价
Close 收盘价
Open 开盘价
Low 最低价

Bearish candle 阴线

Close < Open · 收盘价低于开盘价
High 最高价
Open 开盘价
Close 收盘价
Low 最低价

Color convention above follows the Chinese market (红涨绿跌): red = 阳线 (up), green = 阴线 (down).

⚠ Common misreading

  • 阳线 ≠ "price rose all day" — a bullish candle only means Close > Open. The intraday path may have dipped first and recovered (e.g. 20 → 17 → 22).
  • 阴线 ≠ "price fell all day" — a bearish candle only means Close < Open. The path may have surged first and then given back gains (e.g. 20 → 23 → 17).

✓ The correct mental model

  • A K-line is a result, not a process (K线是结果，不是过程) — it is a "snapshot" of one time period.
  • The candle only reflects the relationship between close and open (收盘价与开盘价的关系).
  • Many different real price paths can produce the exact same OHLC bar.
★Remembering that a candle is only a snapshot helps you avoid many misreadings (能帮你避免很多误解). 阳线不代表一定涨，阴线不代表一定跌。

3Body and Shadows (实体与影线)

A candlestick consists of three parts: the body (实体), the upper shadow (上影线), and the lower shadow (下影线).

阴线Bearish candle anatomy (Close < Open)

  • Upper shadow 上影线: High → Open — the part where price pushed up and fell back (当日冲高回落的部分).
  • Body 实体: Open → Close — the actual result of the bull/bear contest (实际的多空对抗结果).
  • Lower shadow 下影线: Close → Low — the part where price probed down and rebounded (当日下探反弹的部分).
💡 A 阴线 closes below its open — sellers (空方) were stronger, and price overall leaned downward.

阳线Bullish candle anatomy (Close > Open)

  • Upper shadow 上影线: High → Close — the part where price pushed up and was pressed back (冲高回落).
  • Body 实体: Open → Close — the actual result of the bull/bear contest.
  • Lower shadow 下影线: Open → Low — the part where price probed down and was supported (下探回升).
💡 A 阳线 closes above its open — buyers (多方) were stronger, and price overall leaned upward.

Three things to remember (记住这三点)

  • The body represents the final outcome of the bull/bear battle (实体代表多空的最终结果).
  • The upper shadow represents a rally that was sold into (上影线代表冲高被打压).
  • The lower shadow represents a dip that was bought up (下影线代表探底被承接).
★The longer the shadows, the larger the price swings and the more obvious the market disagreement (影线越长，说明价格波动越大，市场分歧越明显). Candlestick shapes reflect market sentiment and the balance of power — they are the basic input of quant strategies.

4Return Rate: What Programs Care About (收益率：程序更重视它)

A price difference and a percentage change are not the same thing (价格差和涨跌幅不是一回事!).

涨跌幅 = (收盘价 − 基准价格)基准价格   →   (19 − 20) / 20 = −0.05 = −5%
  • The percentage change (涨跌幅) is a relative change — it removes the effect of the absolute price level and reflects the true return.
  • Worked example: base price (开盘价) = 20元, close = 19元 → difference = −1元, change = (19−20)/20 = −5%.

Price difference vs percentage change (差价 vs 涨跌幅)

✗ Looking only at the absolute difference

  • Stock A: 100元 → 95元, difference −5元.
  • Stock B: 1000元 → 995元, difference −5元.
  • Both fell 5元 — but the impact is completely different! The higher the price level, the bigger the difference looks, which doesn't mean the return is bigger.

✓ Looking at the percentage change

  • Stock A: 100元 → 95元 = −5%.
  • Stock B: 1000元 → 995元 = −0.5%.
  • A fell much more — higher risk! Relative change is the more important metric in investing.

The underlying logic of quant strategies (量化策略的底层逻辑)

  • Use percentage change to compute returns and drawdowns (使用涨跌幅来计算收益和回撤).
  • It makes stocks at different price levels comparable (不同价格的股票才具有可比性).
  • It is closer to the real profit/loss of your capital (更接近资金的真实盈亏情况).
★Remember: programs care about the return rate, not the price difference (程序更关心收益率，而不是价格差!). The first step of quantitative investing: learn to think in returns (学会用收益率思考).
