Risk Control & the First Backtest Framework

Decide how much you can afford to lose first — then decide how much to buy. (先决定最多能亏多少，再决定买多少！) True risk control isn't about losing a little less after the fact — it's about calculating the worst case before you place the order.

★Overview — Three-Layer Risk Control System

Risk controllable → reasonable positions → long-term survival. The core of quantitative trading is not pursuing high returns, but controlling risk.

3
Account-Level Risk (账户整体风险 · System-level)
Control max drawdown, daily loss limits — prevent catastrophic account damage
2
Position Risk (持仓风险 · Portfolio-level)
Limit single-instrument position ratio — avoid single-stock black swan events
1
Single-Trade Risk (单笔交易风险 · Trade-level)
Control per-trade loss — prevent one trade from blowing up the account

No layer can be bypassed — all three work together to protect account safety. (任何一层都不能绕过，三层共同守护账户安全。)

1Single-Trade Risk — How Much Can You Afford to Lose?

Use a loss you can "tolerate" to work backwards on how many shares to buy. (用一个你能"承受"的亏损，倒推可以买多少股。)

step 1Risk Budget (风险预算) — max loss per trade

Risk Budget (风险预算)= Total Equity (总权益)× Single-Trade Risk % (单笔风险比例)

Recommended range: 0.5%–2%. Too high (>2%) risks severe damage; too low (<0.2%) makes it hard for the strategy to generate returns.

step 2Per-Share Risk (每股风险)

Per-Share Risk (每股风险)= Buy Price (买入价)− Stop-Loss Price (止损价)

step 3Risk-Allowed Quantity (风险允许数量)

Risk-Allowed Qty (风险允许数量)= Risk Budget (风险预算)÷ Per-Share Risk (每股风险)

Worked example (练习数据)

ParameterValueCalculationResult
Total equity1,000,000——
Single-trade risk %1%——
Risk budget—1,000,000 × 1%10,000
Buy price20.00——
Stop-loss price18.00——
Per-share risk—20 − 182.00
Risk-allowed quantity—10,000 ÷ 25,000 shares
💡 Key insight: Before every trade, ask yourself: can I accept the worst-case outcome of this trade? (每一次开仓前，都要知道：这笔交易最坏的结果，我能不能接受？)

2Position Limit — Even if Risk Allows, Don't Buy Too Much

No matter how good the opportunity, control single-instrument exposure. Diversify risk to survive longer. (再好的机会，也要控制单标的仓位，避免一把梭哈。)

step 4Position-Allowed Quantity (仓位允许数量)

Max Position Value (最大仓位金额)= Total Equity (总权益)× Max Position % (最大仓位比例)
Position-Allowed Qty (仓位允许数量)= Max Position Value÷ Buy Price (买入价)

Worked example

ParameterValueCalculationResult
Max position %20%——
Max position value—1,000,000 × 20%200,000
Position-allowed qty—200,000 ÷ 2010,000 shares

Without position control (不控制仓位)

  • Heavy concentration in one instrument
  • One drawdown could wipe out the account
  • Full loss potential (一个回撤，就可能满盘皆输)

With position control (控制仓位)

  • Reasonable single-instrument limits
  • Risk diversified across instruments
  • Controlled drawdowns, long-term profitability (回撤可控，长期才能盈利)

3Final Buy Quantity — Take the Minimum

Not "how much can I buy" but "how much do all constraints allow me to buy". (不是"我能买多少"，而是"所有条件允许我买多少"！)

The min() rule

Final Qty (最终买入数量)= min ( Risk-Allowed, Position-Allowed )

Risk-allowed: 5,000 shares

"This trade can lose at most 10,000 yuan"

Position-allowed: 10,000 shares

"This instrument can be at most 200,000 yuan"

Final = min(5,000, 10,000) = 5,000 shares

Complete calculation summary

StepParameterValue
①Total equity1,000,000
②Single-trade risk %1%
③Risk budget = ① × ②10,000
④Buy price / Stop-loss20.00 / 18.00
⑤Per-share risk = buy − stop2.00
⑥Risk-allowed qty = ③ ÷ ⑤5,000
⑦Max position %20%
⑧Position-allowed qty = (① × ⑦) ÷ buy10,000
⑨Final = min(⑥, ⑧)5,000 shares
💡 Risk control's core purpose is not to maximize returns — it's to prevent one mistake from taking you out of the game. (风险控制的核心不是追求收益最大化，而是避免一次失误就出局。)

4Account-Level Risk — The System Brake

Account-level risk control isn't about one trade — it protects the entire account's long-term survival. (账户级风控不是针对一笔交易，而是为了保护整个账户的长期生存能力。)

systemThree common account-level rules (账户级风控规则)

⚠ Warning line (预警线)

TriggerDaily loss ≥ 2%
ActionStop opening new positions (停止开仓)
WhyPrevent emotional overtrading; avoid expanding today's losses

🛑 Forced line (强制线)

TriggerCurrent drawdown ≥ 10%
ActionCut all positions in half (仓位减半)
WhyReduce risk exposure; give the account time to recover

🚨 Kill switch (暂停策略)

TriggerMax drawdown ≥ 15%
ActionClose all, pause strategy (全部平仓，暂停策略)
WhyStrategy may have failed; stop and wait for new signals

Drawdown formula reminder

Drawdown (回撤)= Peak Equity − Current Equity÷ Peak Equity (最高权益)
Remember: Earning money is skill; surviving is the real ability. Account-level risk control is the last line of defense — it determines whether your account can endure market cycles. (会赚钱是本事，能活下来才是本领！账户级风控是最后一道防线。)

5Stop-Loss: Your Exit Mechanism ≠ Guaranteed Price

Stop-loss is the protection when your trade logic fails — but it doesn't guarantee execution at the stop price. (止损是交易逻辑失效后的保护机制，但不保证一定按止损价成交。)

riskWhy stop-losses can fail (止损可能失效的常见情况)

Gap down (跳空缺口)

Opens below your stop price — fills at a much worse price.

Extreme volatility (剧烈波动)

Price punches through the stop level instantly, no time to fill.

Low liquidity (流动性不足)

Not enough buy-side orders to absorb your sell.

Slippage (滑点影响)

Actual fill price may be worse than expected.

Gap-down example

Plan

Buy price20.00
Stop-loss18.00
Expected max loss2.00 per share

Reality (gap down)

Next day opens at17.00
Actual fill17.00
Actual loss3.00 per share (50% more!)
💡 Day 14's biggest cognitive shift: Stop-loss is a "plan", not a "guarantee". Risk management must consider the worst case. Use stop-losses together with account-level risk controls for layered defense. (止损是"计划"，成交价是"结果"，中间可能有滑点！)
