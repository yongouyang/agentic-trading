**Breakeven after the trough: DCA 2022-11-30 (~6 weeks); lump 2023-05-18

27.1% from Sep 2021) because it bought the bottom with fresh cash.

### Read-through

- Lump sum wins in absolute terms in both windows (+150% / +143% vs +95% /
  +92%) — in a rising market, cash on the sidelines costs returns. The gap
  (~HKD 600–660k) is the price of caution.
- DCA wins on *path quality*: smaller drawdowns, much faster breakeven
  (6 weeks vs 7 months in the stress case), and equal-or-better
  money-weighted returns (XIRR). For money that arrives monthly (salary),
  DCA isn't a choice — and its worst year here was −4 to −6%.
- If holding a lump: history says deploy it, but the psychological cost is
  real — the Jan-2022-peak lump investor spent 7 months underwater.

## 6. Conclusions

- A 4–5 fund portfolio consistent with the user's style:
  **VOO (core) + SMH or VGT (growth satellite, 10–20%) + GLD (diversifier)
  + SCHD (income/value) + 3074.HK or VXUS (non-US)**.
- Variant A dominates on paper but 30% of it (SMH + TW) is one concentrated
  AI-hardware bet with a −45% historical drawdown; the 2021–26 window was
  simultaneously a semi bull and a gold bull, and daily-rebalance math
  flatters returns by ~1%/yr.
- Avoid despite good trailing numbers: XLE (2022 energy shock, not
  repeatable), SLV (one 2026 spike), TLT/BND (lost money over 5Y).
- HK-listed reality: the only HK-listed funds with real 5Y performance are
  Taiwan (3074) and Gold (2840). HK/China home-market indexes returned
  0–4%/yr — consistent with the Phase-7 finding that HK is a stock-picker's
  market (HK/G2 stock-selection signal alive) while the index goes nowhere.
- Caveats: 5 years = one regime; correlations drift toward 1 in crashes;
  historical drawdowns are a floor for expectations, not a ceiling.
