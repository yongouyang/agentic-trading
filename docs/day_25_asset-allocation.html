Multi-Asset Portfolios & Asset Allocation

3 strategies does not mean you're truly diversified — multiple strategies may all buy the same class of assets, leaving risk concentrated (策略分散 ≠ 资产分散). Today we look at where the money actually ends up: which assets and industries, how much weight each gets, and how to control concentration risk. (看清资金最终配置在了哪些资产和行业，识别集中风险，学会做更合理的资产配置！)

1Strategy Diversification ≠ Asset Diversification

Multiple strategies (多个策略) find different opportunities ("怎么赚到钱"). Multi-asset (多标的) means investing in different assets ("钱买到哪里"). Asset allocation (资产配置) decides each asset's weight — "how much to buy, how to balance risk".

example3 strategies that look different — but aren't

StrategyTypeBuys
Strategy ATrend following (趋势跟踪)Tencent (腾讯)
Strategy BMean reversion (均值回归)Alibaba (阿里)
Strategy CEvent driven (事件驱动)Meituan (美团)
⚠️ All three are essentially exposed to Internet/Tech industry risk! Even with 3 different strategies, if they all buy the same class of assets, when that industry falls the whole portfolio suffers together. (本质上都暴露在互联网/科技行业风险中！)

✗ Strategy-diversified, assets NOT diversified (risk concentrated)

Trend → Tencent · Mean reversion → Alibaba · Event driven → Meituan

  • Same industry: Internet/Tech (互联网/科技)
  • When the industry falls, all 3 strategies can lose together!

✓ Strategy-diversified AND asset-diversified (risk reduced)

Trend → Tencent (Tech) · Mean reversion → Ping An (Financials) · Event driven → Sinopec (Energy)

  • Different industries: Tech + Financials + Energy
  • Different return drivers — risks hedge each other!

Why does this matter?

  • Industry risk is real — policy, regulation, competition, technology shifts can drag an entire industry down together.
  • Strategies can't hedge same-source risk — if the assets behind the strategies are the same, their ups and downs are highly synchronized.
  • True diversification = different risk sources — combinations across different assets, regions, and industries are more robust.
One-liner: Strategy diversification is finding opportunities (找机会); asset diversification is controlling risk (控制风险). Only with both is the Portfolio truly robust! Before buying, ask yourself: which industries do my assets belong to? If that industry falls, will my whole portfolio get hurt?

2What Is a Multi-Asset Portfolio?

Only by putting different assets together can you build a true portfolio. A Portfolio isn't just stocks — it can include ETFs, bonds, commodities, cash, and more; together they shape the portfolio's return and risk.

assetsCommon asset types

Stocks (股票)

Higher growth potential, larger volatility (成长性较高, 波动较大)

ETF

Diversified holdings, low cost (分散持仓, 低成本)

Bonds (债券)

Relatively stable returns, smaller volatility

Commodities (商品)

Inflation hedge, low correlation with markets

Cash (现金)

Strong liquidity, lower yield

Benefits of a multi-asset portfolio

Diversify risk (分散风险)

Low correlation between assets — they rarely crash together.

Lower volatility (降低波动)

💡 Remember today: the core of a multi-asset Portfolio is putting assets with different risk characteristics together so they complement each other (互相补充) — not amplify each other's risk!

3What Is Asset Weight? — Bigger Weight, Bigger Impact

A Portfolio assigns each asset a different weight (权重) — the weight determines its "share of voice" in the account.

definitionThe most important formula

Asset weight = market value of the asset ÷ total account equity (资产权重 = 该资产的市值 / 账户总权益)
Example: total account equity = 100万元 Tencent holding value = 20万 → Tencent weight = 20万 / 100万 = 20%

How weight affects the account — suppose the market falls one day

AssetWeightDaily moveImpact on account
Tencent (腾讯)30%−5%−1.50%
Alibaba (阿里)25%−4%−1.00%
沪深300 ETF15%−2%−0.30%
Short-term bond fund (中短债基金)10%+0.02%+0.00%
Gold ETF (黄金ETF)10%+1%+0.10%
Cash (现金)10%0%0.00%
Total100%——−2.70%

The bigger the weight, the bigger the impact on the account even for the same percentage move. High-weight assets matter a lot; low-weight assets matter little.

Scenario A: heavy concentration in one asset

Of 100万, Tencent is 80% (Tencent 80% / Alibaba 5% / ETF 5% / Bonds 5% / Cash 5%).

If Tencent falls 10% in a day, the account loses: 80% × 10% = −8%!

Scenario B: diversified allocation

Weights more balanced (Tencent 20% / Alibaba 20% / ETF 20% / Bonds 20% / Gold 10% / Cash 10%).

Even if Tencent falls 10%, the account impact is only: 20% × 10% = −2%!

💡 Tips: weights drift as prices move — rebalance regularly. New assets and cash flows in/out also change weights. Sensible weight allocation is the first step of risk control.

One-liner: an asset's weight is its "voting right" in your account — the bigger the weight, the bigger the impact. (权重决定了资产对你的账户有多大的话语权！)

4Concentration Risk — One Pit Can Sink the Whole Game

Money overly concentrated in a single asset or industry gets its risk amplified without limit (钱过度集中在单一资产或行业，风险会被无限放大). One piece of bad news can cause a large drawdown.

compareTwo accounts, same 100万 — very different outcomes

High-concentration account (risk high)

  • Tencent 50万 (50%)
  • Alibaba 25万 (25%)
  • Meituan 15万 (15%)
  • Baidu 10万 (10%)

Internet/Tech share: 100%

Diversified account (risk low)

  • Tencent 20万 (20%)
  • Kweichow Moutai (贵州茅台) 20万 (20%)
  • 沪深300 ETF 20万 (20%)
  • Consumer ETF (消费ETF) 20万 (20%)
  • Cash 20万 (20%)

Internet/Tech share: 20%

Same shock — "Internet sector blow-up", sector falls 30% overall

High-concentration account

Tech = 100%, fully affected → loss ≈ 30% (−30万元)

Diversified account

Tech = 20%, only part affected → loss ≈ 6% (−6万元)

💡 The essence of concentration risk: when your money is over-concentrated in one asset or industry, your returns depend heavily on its performance — once it has trouble, the account takes a heavy hit. Sources: single-stock blow-up risk (单只股票暴雷), industry policy risk (行业政策), macro-economic risk (宏观经济).

Common concentration traps

Single stock too large

One stock > 30% → risk clearly rises! (e.g. 60% in one name)

Single industry too large

One industry > 50% → very risky! (e.g. 80% tech)

Style over-concentrated

Only growth or only value stocks → large drawdowns in some cycles.

Correlation too high

Assets move almost identically — no diversification at all!

How to control concentration?

  • ✅ Set a maximum position per single stock (e.g. ≤ 20%)
  • ✅ Set a maximum exposure per industry (e.g. ≤ 40%)
  • ✅ Check regularly and rebalance
  • ✅ Don't put all your eggs in one basket!
Remember this: Diversified investing isn't about buying a lot — it's about never letting one single risk (同一种风险) control the entire account!

5Industry Exposure — See the Hidden Risk Sources

Different stocks may belong to the same industry — their risk stays highly correlated. You may think you bought many stocks, but if they're all in one industry, an industry downturn still hurts the whole portfolio.

example3 Internet stocks vs 3 different industries

Looks diversified, actually concentrated

Tencent (Internet) + Alibaba (Internet) + Meituan (Internet)

  • Industry: Internet/Tech
  • Risk source: almost identical

Truly diversified, different industries

Tencent (Internet) + Kweichow Moutai (白酒/liquor) + Ping An (Financials)

  • Industry: Internet + Consumer + Financials
  • Risk source: relatively independent

💡 Goal: avoid any single industry taking too large a share — let different industries hedge each other's risk as much as possible! (避免单一行业占比过高，尽量让不同行业互相对冲风险！)

Why watch industry exposure?

Industries fall together

Economy, policy, regulation affect an entire industry at once.

Higher correlation

Stocks in the same industry move highly in sync.

Risk not truly diversified

Even with many stocks, portfolio volatility stays large.

Hurts long-term returns

Over-concentration makes long-term performance unstable.

How to control industry exposure?

  • ✅ Set industry caps — e.g. a single industry ≤ 40% of the portfolio
  • ✅ Check industry weights regularly — monthly/quarterly review, adjust promptly
  • ✅ Combine asset types — stocks + bonds + commodities/ETF + cash
  • ✅ Adjust with the market environment — during industry rotation, moderately reduce weights of high-flying industries
One-liner: You're not just buying stocks — you're buying the risk of industries (买的不只是股票，更是行业的风险). Control industry exposure so the portfolio can sail steadier and further through storms! Strategy diversification ≠ industry diversification; see clearly where the money ultimately goes; industry diversification is the key step to lowering portfolio risk.

Today's takeaways: strategy portfolios solve strategy-level risk; asset allocation solves asset-level risk. Simple rules first, complex models later; control risk first, chase returns second. The essence of diversification: never let one single risk control the whole account!

★Day 25 Summary — Building a Healthy Portfolio

Strategy finds opportunities, Portfolio does asset allocation, Risk controls risk. A good portfolio doesn't win by one lucky bet — it wins through scientific allocation, living longer and going further in all market environments.

A top-down risk management framework

Diversify risk layer by layer — only then is the portfolio truly robust! (层层分散风险，组合才真正稳健！)

Core checklist for building a Portfolio

  • ✅ Is any single-stock position too high? (suggested ≤ 20%)
  • ✅ Is any single industry/sector share too high? (suggested ≤ 40%)
  • ✅ Does the portfolio hold different types of assets? (stocks / bonds / commodities / cash …)
  • ✅ Is the cash ratio adequate? (suggested ≥ 10%)
  • ✅ Is portfolio correlation too high? (avoid everything rising and falling together)
  • ✅ Do you rebalance regularly? (bring the portfolio back to a sensible state)

Five key takeaways

  1. Strategy diversification ≠ asset diversification — the real risk may hide in the assets and industries.
  2. The Portfolio's goal is not the highest return — it's long-term returns with risk under control (获得风险可控的长期回报).
  3. Controlling concentration is the key to protecting principal and avoiding major drawdowns.
  4. Asset allocation is a process of continuous optimization — rebalance regularly.
  5. A good portfolio lets you move forward more steadily in an uncertain market!
★Investing is a marathon, not a sprint. With the right allocation, you win in long-term compounding! (投资是一场马拉松，不是短跑冲刺。配置得当，才能在长期复利中赢得胜利！)

Diversified investing is not buying a lot — it's never letting one single risk control the whole account. Control risk first, then talk about returns; survive long, then earn long! (分散投资不是买得多，而是避免让同一种风险控制整个账户！先控制风险，才能谈收益；活得久，才能赚得久！)
