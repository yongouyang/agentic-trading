Factor Backtest — Is This Factor Really Effective?

A factor's quality can't be judged by intuition alone — you must use historical data to verify whether it truly delivers excess returns (超额收益). The essence of backtesting is using history to validate a factor: if it is effective over the long run, it should consistently beat the benchmark (基准). Backtesting is not about proving yourself right — it's about actively seeking evidence of a factor's stable discriminative power.

1Why Must Factors Be Backtested? — Sound Logic ≠ Actually Works in the Market

Many ideas "sound reasonable", but the market may not move the way we think. A backtest uses historical data to examine a factor's past performance and assess its likely future effectiveness.

Ideas we often have (我们常有这样的想法)

  • "Companies with low PE are cheaper — they should rise in the future"
  • "High-ROE companies are higher quality — worth holding long-term"
  • "Stocks that recently rose well may continue their trend"

These logics all sound reasonable — but the market may not follow our script!

Why does sound logic ≠ an effective factor?

  • The market has already priced in the information (价格已经包含预期)
  • The factor worked in the past, but may fail in the future (market environments change)
  • Too many people know the factor → crowding dilutes its effectiveness (有效性被稀释)
  • Short-term luck plays a big role (偶然表现好不代表一直好)
Goal of backtesting (回测的目的): verify whether a factor has stable discriminative power and persistent excess-return ability. (验证因子是否具备稳定的区分能力和持续的超额收益能力)

⚠️ Short-term high returns may be an "illusion" (假象) caused by market style, luck, or a special stretch of market conditions!
What we should focus on instead: multi-dimensional stability (多维度的稳定性)
  • Across time — effective in multiple years and different market environments?
  • Across markets — works in A-shares / HK / US stocks?
  • Across parameters — different group counts or rebalance frequencies, effect still exists?
  • Across stock pools — large caps, small caps, within industries?
  • Statistical stability — IC and long-short returns positive long-term with controlled volatility?
💡 Only multi-dimensional verification increases our confidence in a factor. Backtesting is not about "proving yourself right" — it's about actively seeking evidence of stable, repeatable discriminative power. (只有通过多维度验证，才能增加我们对因子的信心！)

Factor backtest (因子回测) — today

Studies the factor's predictive power — only whether the factor itself works. Focus: group returns, long-short returns, IC, stability.

Strategy backtest (策略回测) — later

Uses the factor to build a strategy — considering buys/sells, position sizing, risk control. Focus: strategy return, drawdown, Sharpe ratio, etc.

💡 Factor backtest ≠ strategy backtest. Factor backtesting is step one — only when a factor proves effective is it worth building a strategy on. We judge discriminative power by observing group returns, long-short returns, and IC. (因子回测是第一步，只有因子有效，才值得继续做策略！)

3Group Returns (分组收益) — Checking Factor Monotonicity

Sort stocks by factor value into groups and observe each group's average future return. Ideally returns decrease monotonically — higher factor value, higher future return.

idealIdeal case: returns monotonically decrease from Q1 to Q5

Assume a value factor predicting next-month returns: Q1 (Top 20%) +3.2% > Q2 +2.1% > Q3 +1.3% > Q4 +0.5% > Q5 (Bottom 20%) −0.4%. Higher factor value → higher future return, with good monotonicity (单调性).

Undesirable: poor monotonicity or reversal

  • Case A — poor monotonicity: +1.2%, +0.3%, +1.6%, −0.2%, −0.5% — Q3 higher than Q1/Q2, no clear pattern
  • Case B — reverse relationship: −0.6%, −0.2%, +0.3%, +1.1%, +2.3% — returns rise as factor value falls

⚠️ Poor monotonicity or reversal suggests the factor may be ineffective — or its direction is simply wrong!

What group returns tell us

  • Discriminative power (区分能力) — the larger the spread between groups, the stronger the factor's discrimination
  • Robustness (稳健程度) — the more stable the monotonicity, the more reliable the factor
  • Direction (方向判断) — is higher better, or lower better?
  • Strategy foundation (策略基础) — an important prerequisite for later portfolio construction

💡 Group returns are one of the most intuitive outputs of factor backtesting. The ideal factor: high factor value → ranked ahead → high future return, i.e. returns with good monotonicity. Group count: 5 or 10 (5 is more common); rebalance monthly/quarterly/semi-annually; holding period 1 week/1 month/3 months. Monotonicity is not an absolute requirement, but the clearer the trend the better. Group returns are only step one — you must also check stability (next section). A single result may contain luck; only sustained observation over many periods reveals whether a factor is truly effective.

4Long-Short Returns (多空收益) — Widening the Gap Between Best and Worst

Long the highest-factor group (Q1), short the lowest (Q5) — the resulting excess return is another key measure of a factor's discriminative power. The higher the long-short return, the stronger the factor.

definitionWhat is long-short return?

Long-short return = Q1 return − Q5 return (做多 Top 组 − 做空 Bottom 组). For the value-factor example: 3.2% − (−0.4%) = +3.6%. Note: the "short" here is mainly for observation and measurement — it does not necessarily mean actually shorting in practice. (注意：这里的"空"更多是为了观察和度量，不一定代表真实去做空。)

Discriminative power (区分能力)

Measures whether the factor can separate future winners from losers — the larger the Q1–Q5 gap, the stronger the discrimination.

Strategy potential (策略潜力)

The higher the long-short return, the greater the theoretical potential of a factor portfolio. The long-short equity curve should trend upward overall.

Stability observation (稳定性观察)

We hope the long-short return stays positive and relatively stable across many periods — long-term stability beats short-term spikes.

Case A: one abnormally high year

2020: 2.1% · 2021: 2.4% · 2022: −1.8% · 2023: −2.3% · 2024: 5.0% · 2025: −1.2%

✗ Cumulative return may look fine, but that doesn't mean the factor works consistently.

Case B: stably positive over time

2020: 2.0% · 2021: 1.8% · 2022: 1.3% · 2023: 1.6% · 2024: 2.2% · 2025: 1.7%

✓ Long-term stable long-short returns are more trustworthy!

💡 Long-short return focuses on the gap between best and worst — only a factor that stays positive long-term has true discriminative power. But higher is not always better: judge it together with group monotonicity, IC, and stability. Observation window: at least 3–5 years for safety; typical rebalance is monthly; common groupings are 5 or 10. History being good ≠ future being good — but if history is bad, be very wary! (历史好 ≠ 未来好，但历史差一定要警惕！)

5IC (Information Coefficient) — Measuring a Factor's Predictive Power

IC checks whether factor rankings match future return rankings — it cares about "is the ordering consistent", not "how much money was made".

definitionWhat is IC?

IC (信息系数, Information Coefficient) measures the correlation between factor ranking at a point in time and future return ranking (usually the Pearson correlation coefficient):

IC = Corr(factor ranking, future return ranking)
  • 😊 IC > 0 — positive correlation: higher factor → higher future return
  • 😐 IC ≈ 0 — no relation between factor and future returns
  • 😠 IC < 0 — negative correlation: higher factor → lower future return

IC time series: stability matters more than any single reading

Example: a factor's weekly IC over the past 3 years (2022–2025, roughly −0.2 to +0.2):

  • Long-term IC > 0 → the factor is effective overall
  • IC fluctuating within a reasonable range → good stability
  • Occasional negative values are normal — the key is long-term behavior

⚠️ Mean IC is not the only criterion

  • ✗ High average IC but extreme volatility → possibly unstable
  • ✗ IC effective in one period, failing in another → environment-sensitive
  • ✗ Look at IC's mean, volatility, stability AND persistence!

💡 Tips (小贴士)

  • Common frequency: weekly IC / monthly IC
  • Common metrics: mean IC, ICIR (IC mean / IC standard deviation)
  • Rule of thumb: ICIR > 0.5–1 is considered quite good
💡 IC measures a factor's "ranking ability" — whether it can predict the ordering of future returns. A factor with a long-term robust positive IC is worth deep research. Backtesting is not about finding the "most profitable factor" but the one that is truly predictive and stable. (长期稳健的正 IC，才是一个值得深入研究的因子！)

6Stability Matters More Than a Single High Return

Whether a factor is truly effective must withstand the test of time and environment. Earning a lot in one backtest doesn't make a good factor — long-term stability is what's reliable. (一次回测赚很多，不一定是好因子；长期稳定才可靠！)

How to test factor stability — five dimensions

#DimensionQuestions to ask
1Cross-year (跨年份稳定性)Effective in different years? Consistent across bull / bear / sideways markets? Long-term long-short return persistently positive?
2Cross-market (跨市场稳定性)Works in different markets — A-shares / HK / US, emerging / developed? Or only effective in a single market?
3Parameter (参数稳定性)Changing group count, rebalance frequency, factor computation window — does the effect survive small parameter changes?
4Stock pool (股票池稳定性)Different pools (whole market / within industries), different sample sizes (500/1000/2000) — consistent results, or dependent on a specific sample?
5Portfolio construction (组合构建稳定性)Different construction methods (equal weight / cap weight / industry neutral) — still robust after switching methods?

Reality check: styles rotate — factors need long-term validation

A-share style example: 2020 成长占优 (growth led), 2021 质量占优, 2022 价值占优, 2023 低估值占优, 2024 价值/质量, 2025 风格轮动 (rotation). If a factor only works when "growth leads", a style switch kills it — a truly good factor should traverse cycles. The same factor's rolling 1-year long-short return differs visibly across style environments.

Practical advice (实操建议)

  • Backtest at least 5 years, covering bull, bear, and sideways markets
  • Test multiple markets — avoid over-relying on one market's style
  • Vary parameters and construction methods to ensure robustness
  • Watch drawdowns and volatility, not just returns
  • Keep tracking the factor's performance on the latest data

🎯 Our goal: find a factor that can stably beat the benchmark over the long term!

💡 The more stable a factor, the more it deserves a place in the portfolio; an unstable factor can fail at any moment even with high historical returns; if multiple factors in a portfolio are simultaneously stable, the portfolio is more reliable; continuous tracking and regular review are keys to quant investing success. Backtesting is not about finding "the most money made" — it's about finding the most trustworthy patterns (最值得信任的规律).

★Summary — What Should a Factor Worthy of Further Research Look Like?

Factor backtesting is not the finish line — it's the start of screening. Only a factor meeting these traits deserves to move to the next step. (因子回测不是终点，而是筛选的开始)

1. MONOTONICITY

Group returns are monotonic

Ideal: higher factor → higher future return (e.g. Q1 +3.2% > Q2 +2.1% > Q3 +1.3% > Q4 +0.5% > Q5 −0.4%). Monotonically decreasing returns prove discriminative power. Bad signs: unordered returns; only extreme groups high with middle groups chaotic; alternating signs with no pattern.

2. LONG-SHORT RETURN

Long-term positive with sufficient magnitude

Long-short = Q1 − Q5. Ideal traits: long-term positive (most periods > 0); controlled drawdowns, no frequent large negative swings; magnitude large enough to be economically meaningful. Long-short return embodies the factor's practical value at the portfolio level.

3. IC

Long-term positive and stable

IC = correlation between factor ranking and future return ranking. Ideal: IC > 0 long-term; mean significantly positive (e.g. > 0.02); moderate volatility, no frequent failures. IC is the factor's "gold-content metric" (含金量指标).

4. STABILITY

Stable across time and environments

Example long-short returns 2020–2025: +6.1%, +4.3%, −1.2%, +3.8%, +5.6%, +2.0%. Performs in bull, bear, and sideways markets; doesn't depend on one year or one market wave; controlled drawdowns — can "survive" every phase. Stability beats short-term high returns!

5. ROBUSTNESS

Not over-sensitive to risks and noise

① Parameter-insensitive (e.g. 5 groups → 10 groups still works) · ② Rebalance-frequency-insensitive (monthly / quarterly / bi-weekly all similar) · ③ Pool-insensitive (whole market vs CSI 300 vs mid-caps) · ④ Not reliant on a few extreme stocks (remove top 5% extreme names, effect roughly unchanged) · ⑤ Not overfit — effective out of sample, not just "making money in history" (e.g. stays effective under time-split backtests). Overfitting signals: great history but terrible out-of-sample; fails the moment parameters change; fails in a different time window; only extreme groups profit while middle groups are chaotic.

6. LOGIC

Sound economic logic or industry explanation

A factor is not a "mysterious formula" — it's a distillation of market patterns. Ask yourself: why might this factor work? What economic behavior or market feature does it reflect? Is there theoretical or empirical support? Is it sustainable in practice? Factors backed by logic survive longer!

7. scoringComposite evaluation — selecting "good factors"

Score each factor on five axes (radar): group-return monotonicity (分组收益单调性), long-short return magnitude (多空收益幅度), IC stability (IC 稳定性), cross-time stability (跨时间稳定性), parameter robustness (参数鲁棒性), plus economic-logic interpretability (经济逻辑可解释性).

ScoreMeaning

4较好 — usable

2较差 — high risk
1很差 — not recommended

Composite score ≥ 4 → may proceed to strategy portfolio testing.

★Factor backtesting is not about "finding a money-making factor" — it's about finding a truly effective one. Only factors with discriminative power, stability, and logical support deserve strategy construction and real capital. (只有具备区分能力、稳定性和逻辑支撑的因子，才值得你去构建策略、投入真金白银！)

Factor research is a marathon, not a 100-meter sprint. Slow is fast — stability is the key to victory. (因子研究是一场马拉松，不是一场百米冲刺。慢就是快，稳才是胜利的关键！)
