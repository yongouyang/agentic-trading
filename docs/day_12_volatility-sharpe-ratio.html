Volatility, Sharpe Ratio & Risk-Adjusted Returns

High returns don't mean a good strategy! (收益高 ≠ 策略好！) Comparing returns alone is not enough — you must also evaluate volatility, Sharpe ratio, and maximum drawdown to determine which strategy is truly worth holding.

Core takeaway: High returns don't equal a good strategy. You must also look at how much volatility was endured to achieve those returns. The pursuit is "high returns + low volatility" — that combination. (收益高不等于策略好，追求的是"高收益 + 低波动"的组合。)

1Volatility — The Bumpiness of the Ride

Higher return volatility means bigger account swings — and a less predictable outcome. (收益率波动越大，账户的起伏也越大！)

riskWhat is volatility? (什么是波动率？)

Volatility (波动率) measures the degree of fluctuation in returns. Higher volatility means bigger swings; lower volatility means a smoother curve. (波动率越高，波动越大；波动率越低，曲线越平稳。)

Two strategies compared (两种策略的收益曲线对比)

Strategy A: Low volatility (低波动)

  • Smooth, steady upward curve (收益曲线平稳向上)
  • Small swings, better holding experience (波动小，持有体验更好)
  • More predictable outcomes

Strategy B: High volatility (高波动)

  • Wild ups and downs (收益曲线大起大落)
  • Large swings, poor holding experience (波动大，持有体验差)
  • Unpredictable, anxiety-inducing

What high volatility means (波动率高意味着什么)

Big account swings (账户波动大)

Net value fluctuates violently.

Poor experience (持有体验差)

Easy to panic, hard to hold.

Unstable results (结果不稳定)

High uncertainty in outcomes.

Potentially larger drawdowns (可能伴随更大回撤)

Historically deeper declines.

💡 Important nuance: High volatility doesn't mean the strategy will definitely lose money! Some high-volatility strategies still deliver excellent long-term returns. But volatility is a key "risk size" indicator — and different strategies can be compared using volatility to match your risk tolerance. (波动率高不代表一定亏损！但波动率是衡量"风险大小"的重要指标之一。)

2Sharpe Ratio — Return Quality per Unit of Risk

The Sharpe Ratio is the key metric for measuring "return quality" — how much excess return you earn per unit of volatility risk. (用来衡量"收益质量"的重要指标！)

Sharpe Ratio (夏普比率)= Strategy Return − Risk-free Rate÷ Volatility (波动率)

For beginner purposes: Sharpe ≈ Annualized Return / Annualized Volatility (初学阶段可近似)

metricFormula breakdown (公式拆解)

ComponentMeaning
Strategy return (策略收益率)The strategy's annualized return level
Risk-free rate (无风险收益率)E.g. government bond yield, typically very low
Volatility (波动率)Degree of return fluctuation (risk magnitude)
Key interpretation (关键理解) Sharpe ↑ = earning more excess return while taking less volatility risk.

The higher the Sharpe, the better the "return per unit of risk" — the strategy's return quality is higher.

Sharpe ratio rough scale (夏普比率粗略理解)

Average (一般)

⚠ But watch for overfitting!
💡 A Sharpe above 3 is exceptional — but be vigilant about overfitting risk. Extremely high backtest Sharpe ratios often don't survive into live trading. (>3 非常优秀，但要警惕过拟合风险！)

3Sharpe Ratio vs Max Drawdown

Both are risk metrics, but they measure different things. You need both — they don't replace each other.

Sharpe Ratio (夏普比率)

  • Measures "return / volatility tradeoff" (收益/波动的性价比)
  • Focuses on day-to-day volatility (日常波动的大小)
  • Reflects long-term return quality (长期收益质量)
  • Higher is better

Max Drawdown (最大回撤)

  • Measures "worst historical decline" (历史最惨的一次下跌)
  • Focuses on peak-to-trough drop (从历史高点到低点的最大跌幅)
  • Reflects extreme risk and capital loss (极端风险和资金损失)
  • Lower is better
Remember! You can't evaluate a strategy by Sharpe alone or by MDD alone. You must comprehensively assess return quality + risk tolerance together! (策略不能只看 Sharpe，也不能只看最大回撤，要综合评价收益质量 + 风险承受能力！)

4Practice — Strategy A vs Strategy B

Higher returns must be better, right? Let's look at the data. (收益更高的一定更好吗？我们用数据说话！)

exerciseHead-to-head comparison

Strategy A

Sharpe (approx.)18% / 9% ≈ 2.0

Higher risk-adjusted return. Smoother ride.

Annualized volatility (年化波动率)20%
Sharpe (approx.)25% / 20% ≈ 1.25

Higher absolute return, but much more volatile.

Verdict (结论) B earns more in absolute terms — but A has a higher risk-adjusted return (Sharpe), meaning A delivers better return quality with more stability. A is more suitable for long-term holding.

(B赚得更多，但A的风险调整后收益更高，收益质量更稳定，更值得长期持有。)
