Objective & Direction

The charter for this project: what it is for, what it is not, the principles it operates on, where it actually stands after six months of building, and the staged direction it heads toward next — each stage entered and exited by a pre-registered gate.

Mission. Replace ad-hoc stock picking with a system that finds candidates, manages risk, executes with discipline, and improves only on evidence — so that every claim this project makes about an edge is one it has tried, and failed, to falsify first.
Reconciled against the built system: docs/architecture-v1.md, docs/phase-0…5-plan.md, PROGRESS.md · 2026-09-12

1What this is, and what it is not

Scope discipline first — every later claim depends on it

This is a personal research instrument, not a product, not a fund, not a broker. 它是一个人的研究工具：每天跑，出清单，带证据和风险；下单的手是人。

It is

  • An agent-assisted stock picker. A daily pipeline that screens a liquid universe, deep-dives the survivors with an LLM, and emits a ranked watchlist with evidence and named risks.
    architecture-v1 §1
  • Two lanes only: HK and US stocks/ETFs. US exposure in the HK lane goes through HK-domiciled index trackers to avoid US estate tax.
    architecture-v1 §2, §6
  • Manual trading. The output is read by a human who places the orders.
    architecture-v1 §9
  • A measurement machine that happens to trade. The distinguishing asset is the ability to decide, not the daily list.

It is not

  • Not a dealer. No broker integration and no order execution in v1. Position tracking exists only as manual trade-journal attribution (journal:link — a CSV in, a decision-versus-list report out; FIFO lots, open positions marked to market): analytics, not portfolio management, and no orders.
    architecture-v1 §9; packages/quant-core/src/journal.ts
  • Not intraday. Daily bars only; installed runs at 16:50 HKT (HK) and 06:10 HKT (US), plus a guarded 20:30 HKT catch-up slot — six jobs, not two.
    architecture-v1 §5, §5.1; scripts/launchd/*.plist
  • Not a black box. Deterministic quant core is the trusted layer; agents propose, the quant core disposes. Every verdict is auditable to its exact prompt.
  • Not a promise of alpha. Six months in, no edge is validated. §5 states what is actually known.

The end-state, stated concretely enough to be falsified

2.1 The system-level objective

The operating objective: assemble the layers into one runnable system, each layer doing exactly one job and passing its output upward.

Use data and rules to find opportunity, manage risk, and execute — then iterate continuously for stable long-run return. 用数据和规则去发现机会、管理风险、执行交易，并通过持续迭代获得长期稳定的收益。

Verify, falsify, replace — never tune to the past.

2.3 The objective that actually distinguishes this project

Build the machine that can tell you whether an edge exists — before risking money on the belief that it does. A momentum or Sharpe ranking is reproducible by anyone. A pre-registered, falsifiable verdict about whether a signal works, with the bar locked before the numbers are seen, is the thing this project actually built. 任何人的排名都能复制；这个项目真正造出来的，是「事先锁死标准、再看结果」的裁决能力。
North Star metric
Time-to-verdict at pre-registered power — days / daysNeeded

Not return. Not Sharpe. The project's success is measured by how fast it converts a hypothesis into a verdict class it can trust. Returns are evidence about the world; the verdict is evidence about our own claims about the world — and only the second one is fully in our control. Every direction choice in §6 is justified by whether it shortens time-to-verdict or lengthens it.

Immediate consequence: at ~10 deep-dive verdicts per lane per day, a modest IC of 0.10 is ~65 months away; at 40 the Phase-5 power table says ~15 months for ~4× the tokens — and that arithmetic drove the measurement-breadth decision (SCREEN_PARAMS.topN 15 → 40).
docs/phase-5-plan.md (power table)

Two corrections to that arithmetic, both measured. (1) Real slot supply is 56 % — 5 of 9 expected slots per lane since 2026-09-01 — which re-prices the Phase-5 clock to ~280 sessions ≈ 13.3 months and Phase 4c's US lane from 1,194 to ~2,132 sessions ≈ 8.4 years. (2) HK cannot supply 40 names. Its post-gate eligible breadth is ~25, and the lane is actually delivering ~2–3 verdicts per run (6 runs → 14 awaiting, 14 late-excluded) against US's ~17 per run. The pooled 40/lane projection is therefore unreachable on the HK half — breadth is a real constraint, not just a token budget.

3Operating principles

Each principle with the measured bill for having ignored it

These are the principles this project operates on, and they are held to the same standard as everything else: where a principle bit hard, the bill is recorded in the last column. A principle you have not yet paid for is still just a slogan.
PrincipleWhat it demandsWhat it cost here when tested
A strategy is a hypothesis Understand the market first, translate it into executable rules, then validate — and be able to say why you make money. H1 was written as a hypothesis with a numeric bar and tested as shipped. It came back insufficient_evidence rather than being quietly deleted.
Look-ahead bias is the deadliest sin Point-in-time replay only; a signal may use nothing the decision moment did not have. The promptness gate found the production pipeline was producing late verdicts — first measured as 24 of 45 (53 %), now 24 of 88 (27 %) as the sample grows. A Sunday catch-up was judging Friday's session with weekend news. All were excluded before any forward label existed.
docs/phase-5-plan.md, amendment 2; PROGRESS 2026-09-12
Costs decide Fees and slippage are part of the strategy, not a footnote; high turnover is a strategy with a hole in it. HK turnover 26.1× against 23 bp per side ≈ 6.0 %/yr drag — most of HK's −27 pp differential on its own. US: 30.9× at 5 bp ≈ 1.5 %/yr. An earlier version quoted the 46 bp round trip against turnover, which double-counts; the per-side convention is the corrected one.
architecture-v1 §9; phase-4b-plan.md
Prefer plateaus to peaks Seek stable regions, not historical optima; simple, stable, explainable beats optimal. The nine-combo weight sweep is a smooth monotone plateau with no isolated spike — and the shipped weights rank 5/9 (US), 6/9 (HK). It may not be used to change the parameters without a new pre-registered test.
Stability > a single high return A factor worthy of research is stable across time, not strong once. US IC was +0.0185 / +0.0316 / +0.0343 in 2023/24/25 but −0.0469 in 2022 and −0.0208 in 2026 — regime-dependent. That is the honest shape of the evidence.
Survive first, profit second Three-layer risk control: per-trade risk, position limits, account-level brake. The risk model sets state, not signal. Cost model and equity/drawdown metrics shipped. Live risk control is per-name eligibility rejects on the signal path (volMax 0.6, mddMin −0.5), not state: there is no account-level brake in the code. This is the largest gap between principle and practice.
Diversification is about correlation More strategies ≠ better diversification; weight determines risk exposure; concentration is the hidden killer. Lane breadth is the binding constraint on evidence: post-gate eligible breadth is 180 (US) and 25 (HK) — and the trend filter, not liquidity, rejects first for 81.2 % of US rejections.
One variable at a time, decided in advance Nuisance parameters may shape design; they may never set a bar. Codified as an explicit firewall on the spent window (Phase 4c). The 0.02 Gate-1 bar's own power floor (US 0.0298 / HK 0.0493) exceeds the bar — a calibration defect found and relabelled, not re-barred.
One more principle, earned here rather than inherited: a descriptive number is not evidence. A top-N-vs-rest spread of +0.83 % at 20d on 60 % positive days was originally called "economically meaningful"; it is a point estimate with no interval, and 60 % over ~49 effectively independent days is a z of about 1.4. It was retracted as stated. This is now the standard applied to everything in the repo.

5.2 The honest one-paragraph position

After roughly six months of building, this project has no validated edge, a validated measurement machine, and one unmeasured differentiator. That is not failure — it is the state that lets a future verdict be believed. The alternative, which most personal quant projects take, is to never build the measurement machine and therefore never learn anything except the backtest's opinion of itself.

What we know we do not have

  • A ranking rule with an edge that survives costs (L4).
  • A portfolio rule that beats holding the index (L5, US).
  • Any evidence at all about the LLM layer (L9).
  • A live risk gate that acts on drawdown (L6) — a principle held in design, not in code.

What we know we do have

  • The ability to replay production point-in-time and emit a verdict class with a Newey–West interval (L8).
  • Data clean enough that a verdict would mean something, in two independent feeds (L1).
  • An audit trail from every verdict back to the exact prompt and model that produced it (L9).
  • Operations that fail loudly and catch up after sleep (L10).

6Direction: what we head toward next

Four stages, each with an entry gate and an exit gate — and no stage that can be entered by wanting it more

The direction is not "find a winning strategy". It is: spend the next period trying to kill the project's best remaining hypothesis, under pre-registered rules, and let the outcome choose the product. 方向不是「找到赚钱策略」，而是「用事先锁定的规则，去杀死自己最好的假设」，让结果决定产品形态。

STAGE 1Make the differentiator measurable NOW → ~13 MONTHS AT CURRENT SUPPLY

The only work that is allowed: work that shortens time-to-verdict or improves the read.

Work in parallel
  • Track A — vendor lane (deciding). Build the loader, measure the design half, lock the bar numerically, spend the test half once. Pre-registered as H1-generality: fresh names on the spent regime — never "validated on fresh data".
  • Track B — prospective differential. Keeps accruing in the background. No action, no decisions from it for years.
  • Phase 5 — H2, the LLM layer. Deep-dive at 40 names/lane; IC | rank decides (not raw IC); readiness rule SE(mean IC) ≤ IC_target / 2.487 at target 0.10; report days / daysNeeded until then.
  • Product work permitted: anything that improves measurement or the read — breadth, cost tracking, the risk-model wiring (L6), UI provenance headers, and two named defects from §5.3: the missing source: "chain" filter on the deciding sample (do it now, while no label exists) and one written economic mechanism per score component.
  • Product work forbidden: any change to factor weights, gate thresholds, universe rules, or the portfolio rule.
  • Open question Stage 1 must answer: HK's post-gate breadth is ~25, so the 40/lane target is unreachable there and the pooled power arithmetic overshoots. Either accept a lower HK breadth and re-state the pooled projection honestly, or pre-register a different HK treatment — but it may not be papered over by a projection that assumes names the lane cannot supply.
Exit gate
  • Track A emits a verdict class: supported · falsified · insufficient_evidence · underpowered · inconclusive.
  • Phase 5 reaches readiness for at least one lane, or the 1.5× SE-stability guard trips to inconclusive.
  • Cap discipline: if the vendor lane's 0.8-power bar exceeds the 0.03 cap, it is declared underpowered by design — before any test-half number is computed. An underpowered lane cannot be rescued by quietly raising the claim.

Why this shape: the deep-dive is the part of the product that is not a commodity. A momentum/Sharpe ranking is reproducible by anyone; a grounded, risk-enumerating verdict is the thing this project built. The layer with zero evidence is the layer with the most value at stake.

STAGE 3Paper, then small real capital GATED ON A SURVIVING VERDICT

Entry gate
  • At least one layer carries a supported verdict at target power, and Gate-2 was not falsified at the costs actually paid.
  • The risk model (L6) is wired as a live state gate, not a feature: volatility ≤ 18 %, MDD ≤ 25 % → de-risk or pause, beta 0.8–1.2, cash ≥ 5 %, single name ≤ 5 %, sector ≤ 30 %.
  • Cost accounting is live and measured, not modelled: fee 0.03 %/side, slippage 0.02–0.10 %, because HK's falsification came from exactly this line.
Doing it in the right order
  • Paper first — same pipeline, same costs, no capital, minimum one full rebalance cycle.
  • Then small real capital, manual execution, with the dashboard's provenance headers telling you which rules produced the list.
  • Attribution on every cycle: which layer earned it, what the costs took, what the benchmark would have done instead. Losing to the index must be visible in the same view as winning.
  • Nothing about this stage involves leverage. Leverage does not create skill.

STAGE 4Automate execution — only if Stage 3 is boring NOT BEFORE

Broker integration (Futu/moomoo or IBKR, both covering HK and US) is explicitly out of v1 and should stay out until manual execution has been profitable, unremarkable, and fully attributable for several cycles. Automating an unvalidated edge multiplies the size of a mistake rather than the size of a return.

architecture-v1 §2, §9 — broker choice recorded, integration deliberately deferred.

What we will not do, at any stage

  • Not tune SCREEN_PARAMS against a window a test has already printed. The plateau finding (shipped weights rank 5/9 US, 6/9 HK) exists precisely to make this temptation explicit.
  • Not add new factors while the current ones remain unread — more hypotheses do not shorten time-to-verdict, they multiply it.
  • Not re-bar after outcomes, loosen a cap, or raise a claim because a lane turned out underpowered.
  • Not cite a descriptive spread as confirmation. No interval, no evidence.
  • Not treat the vendor archive as fresh data. It is fresh names on a spent regime, and is pre-registered as that and nothing more.
  • Not widen the universe to buy power — lowering a liquidity floor to admit more names is forbidden (that option was declined for HK: 18.1 y → ~8 y). Widening measurement depth inside the existing universe is a different act: Phase-5's 15 → 40 was user-locked, label-free, and disclosed. It is still a power purchase, and it is recorded as one rather than presented as a product decision.
  • Not let the LLM layer touch authoritative state. Agents propose; the quant core disposes.
  • Not start Stage 3 or 4 on the strength of a backtest alone.

8Working rules

How this project makes claims — the discipline that is the actual product

Pre-registration

  • Forks are decided and written into a phase-N-plan.md before any outcome number exists. Dated amendments are allowed while no label exists; after labels, no.
  • Firewall: the spent window's outcome statistics may inform design (nuisance parameters — SE, breadth, IR) but may never set, loosen, or justify a bar.
  • Caps exist to protect the claim: the 0.03 bar cap and the 1.5× SE-stability guard both convert a weak design into an honest verdict class instead of an inflated one.

Verdict vocabulary

  • supported · falsified · insufficient_evidence · underpowered · inconclusive — fixed, and reused across rounds so results stay comparable.
  • insufficient_evidence is the default reading, not a failure to explain. Naming it correctly is the whole point of the vocabulary.
  • A FAIL cannot falsify an effect the lane could never have detected — that distinction is what separates falsified from insufficient_evidence.

Evidence hygiene

  • Point-in-time or it does not count. The promptness gate is a permanent feature: a verdict formed later than the session it screened is not a prospective observation.
  • Descriptive is not evidence. Point estimates need intervals; a t below 2 is suggestive at best, and saying so is the standard.
  • Publish the retraction. Corrected errors stay in the record — the −27 pp HK figure, the "unpassable bar", the double-counted round trip, the "economically meaningful" spread. Corrections are the audit trail working.
  • Provenance in the read itself, and in anything that decides. Every dashboard list carries a data-integrity header (screened/excluded/degraded, effective cutoff) and a rule-provenance line naming which unvalidated rules produced the ranking. The same pin applies to deciding statistics: a sample that admits operator-selected runs is a selection, not a sample — so the deep-dive sample requires source: "chain" and counts what it excludes. Learned the hard way twice, in the UI and then in the statistic.

Build discipline

  • Agents propose, the quant core disposes. The LLM never touches authoritative state; structured outputs, pinned temperature, a persistent decision log keyed by prompt hash.
  • Fail visibly. Typed outcomes rather than silent exclusion; two independent degraded triggers; ops:health as the post-condition of the two daily chains (the weekly sentinel and F10 jobs are covered only indirectly, through the artifact checks inside computeHealth — so "every job" would be an overstatement); catch-up that checks the verdict leg, not just the screen.
  • Model tiering: deep tier with high thinking for design forks and measurement design; fast tier for execution once decisions are locked.
    AGENTS.md — switch models explicitly when the phase changes.
  • One writer, tested invariants. Byte-deterministic prompt builders, W2's crashed-run invariant, 497 + 1 skipped tests.
The rules are the asset. Any of the ten layers can be replaced — a better data feed, a different model, another factor. The one thing that must not be replaced is the habit of deciding what would count as evidence before looking, because that habit is what makes the next six months different from the last six.
