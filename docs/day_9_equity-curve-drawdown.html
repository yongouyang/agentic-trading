Equity Curve, Cumulative Return & Max Drawdown

How much did you earn, and how much pain did you endure along the way? (赚了多少，以及中途承受了多大的亏损？) A strategy must be evaluated not just on returns, but on the suffering endured to achieve them.

★Overview — Four Pillars of Performance

Today's goal: understand and compute Equity Curve, Cumulative Return, Current Drawdown, and Maximum Drawdown — and grasp the relationships between them.

equity curve

Equity Curve (收益曲线)

How total equity changes over time — the full story of the strategy's performance.

return

Cumulative Return (累计收益)

The final answer: how much money was made (or lost) relative to the start.

drawdown

Current Drawdown (当前回撤)

How far we've fallen from the all-time high — measures current pain.

risk

Max Drawdown (最大回撤)

The worst drawdown ever — measures the deepest pain the strategy inflicted.

Core insight: A strategy cannot be judged by returns alone. You must also look at how much drawdown was endured to achieve those returns. Returns and drawdowns coexist — they are not mutually exclusive. (收益与回撤可以同时存在，并不冲突。一个策略不能只看赚了多少，还要看为了赚这些钱，曾经承受过多大的回撤。)

1Equity Curve — The Strategy's Biography

The equity curve plots total equity over time — it is the most fundamental chart in quant investing. (收益曲线就是账户总权益随时间变化所连成的曲线。)

coreWhat is an Equity Curve? (什么是收益曲线？)

  • Each day, compute Equity = Cash + position market value (每天计算账户的总权益).
  • Plot these points in time order and connect them into a curve.
  • X-axis = time, Y-axis = equity.
  • Shows how the account grows, declines, recovers, and oscillates over the entire period.
Why is it important?
  • See how the account grows or falls over time.
  • Spot volatility, drawdowns, and recovery patterns.
  • Shows the real performance process — not just "how much was earned" but "how it was earned" (不只是最终赚了多少，更看重"怎么赚的").

Example: 7-day equity curve (示例：某策略7天的收益曲线)

💡 The equity curve is one of the most fundamental and important charts in quantitative investing. It lets us intuitively see the strategy's performance across the time dimension. (收益曲线是量化投资中最基础、最核心的图表之一，它让我们直观地看到：策略在时间维度上的表现如何。)

2Cumulative Return — The Final Verdict

Cumulative return measures how much money was ultimately made (or lost) relative to the starting capital. (累计收益衡量的是：最终赚了多少钱。)

Cumulative Return (累计收益率)= Final Equity − Initial Equity÷ Initial Equity

metricThree possible outcomes (三种结果)

Profit case (盈利)

Initial100,000
Final120,000
Return+20.00%

Break-even (回本)

Initial100,000
Final100,000
Return0.00%

Loss case (亏损)

Initial100,000
Final80,000
Return−20.00%
💡 Cumulative return only looks at the result — it doesn't care about the journey in between. Two strategies can have the same final return but wildly different paths! (累计收益只看"结果"，不关心中间经历了多少波动和回撤。两个策略最终收益相同，但过程可能天差地别！)
Remember: Cumulative return answers "how much was earned in the end?" — it is the core metric for the final result. But looking at return alone is not enough; you must also check drawdowns and volatility! (仅看累计收益还不够，还要看回撤和波动！)

3Current Drawdown — How Far From the Peak?

Current drawdown measures how much equity has fallen from the all-time high. (当前回撤：从历史最高权益回落了多少。)

Current Drawdown (当前回撤)= Current Equity − Peak Equity÷ Peak Equity (历史最高权益)

riskInterpreting the result (如何理解结果)

ResultMeaning
NegativeCurrently in drawdown — equity is below the peak (处于回撤中)
ZeroEquity has returned to the peak — no drawdown (回到历史高点)
PositiveNew all-time high — no drawdown (创出新高，无回撤)

Example (举个例子)

Calculation Peak equity: 130,000 (13万)
Current equity: 100,000 (10万)

Current drawdown = (100,000 − 130,000) ÷ 130,000 = −23.08%
→ Currently 23.08% below the all-time high. (表示当前比历史高点回落了23.08%)
💡 Drawdown measures "how far from the peak", not "how much money was lost". Even if you haven't lost money overall, as long as you haven't returned to the peak, you are still in a drawdown! (回撤衡量的是"离最高点有多远"，而不是"亏了多少钱"。即使您目前不亏钱，只要没回到最高点，仍然可能处于回撤中！)

4Maximum Drawdown (MDD) — The Worst Pain Ever

Maximum drawdown is the single most severe drawdown across all history — the deepest valley the strategy ever fell into. (最大回撤：在所有历史回撤中，最严重的一次回撤。)

riskHow to compute MDD (计算思路)

  1. Find every historical peak (找到历史上每一个"高点").
  2. Calculate the drawdown from each peak to every subsequent trough (计算从该高点回落到之后的每一个低点的回撤).
  3. The most negative value across all drawdowns is the MDD (所有回撤中，最小/最负的值，就是最大回撤).
Drawdown= Current Equity − Peak Equity÷ Peak Equity → MDD = min(all drawdowns)

Worked example (举个例子)

Equity sequence (万)PeakDrawdownNote
10 → 12 → 11 → 9 → 13 → 10———
Peak 1 = 12, trough = 912(9−12)/12 = −25%Drawdown 1
Peak 2 = 13, trough = 1013(10−13)/13 ≈ −23.08%Drawdown 2
MDD = min(−25%, −23.08%)−25%

Why is MDD so important? (为什么最大回撤很重要？)

What MDD tells you

  • Measures the maximum pain endured to earn returns (为了赚取收益，策略曾经让你承受的最大痛苦有多大)
  • One of the core risk control metrics (风险控制的核心指标之一)
  • Investors and institutions care deeply about MDD (投资者和机构非常关注最大回撤)

Even if profitable, high MDD can cause

  • Psychological breakdown (心理崩溃)
  • Forced stop-loss (被迫止损)
  • Capital tied up too long (资金被占用过久)
★MDD is not the final loss — it is the deepest drawdown the strategy experienced during its run. It reflects the strategy's "risk exposure" and "holding experience". When choosing a strategy, returns matter — but MDD matters just as much! (最大回撤不是最终亏损，而是策略在运行过程中经历的最深的一次回撤。它反映了策略的"风险暴露"与"持有体验"。选策略时，收益重要，最大回撤同样关键！)

5Returns & Drawdowns Coexist

Even if the final return is zero, the strategy may have experienced enormous drawdowns along the way. (即使最终没赚钱，中途也可能经历过很大的回撤。)

insightThe classic example (经典例子)

Initial capital100,000
Historical peak130,000
Current capital100,000
Cumulative return= (10−10)/10= 0%
Current drawdown= (10−13)/13= −23.08%
Core insight (核心认知) The account returned to its starting capital — that doesn't mean there was no drawdown!

Drawdown reflects the pain of the journey. The account went from 100k → 130k → back to 100k — a 23% round-trip of suffering, even though the "result" is 0%.

Account back to start ≠ no drawdown!
(账户回到初始资金，不代表没有回撤！回撤反映的是过程中的痛苦。)

Three dimensions of evaluation (三个评价维度)

Cumulative Return (累计收益)

  • Measures final result — how much was earned
  • Higher is better
  • But doesn't show the journey

Current Drawdown (当前回撤)

  • Measures current pain — how far from peak
  • Lower is better
  • Reflects current risk state

Max Drawdown (最大回撤)

  • Measures worst experience — deepest pain ever
  • Lower is better
  • Evaluates risk tolerance requirements
★Cumulative return tells you the "result". Current drawdown tells you "how painful right now". Maximum drawdown tells you "how bad the worst time was". An excellent strategy should earn solid returns while keeping drawdowns under control! (一个优秀的策略，应该在获得可观收益的同时，控制好回撤！)
