You may be right on direction, but still lose a lot of money — why? (看对方向，为什么还是可能亏很多？) The strategy decides what to do; position size decides how much you bet. (策略决定做什么，仓位决定押多少。)

1What is Position?

Position determines how many chips you've placed on the table. (仓位决定你在市场中押了多少筹码。)

Position (仓位)= Capital deployed (投入市场的资金)÷ Total equity (账户总权益)× 100%

20%

50%
50% reserve

Balance between offense and defense. Can attack or retreat.

100% — Full position (满仓)

100%

Maximum returns, but also maximum risk exposure. All capital at risk.

Common position terminology (常见的仓位表示)

TermMeaning
100% — Full position (满仓)All account capital is in the market
50% — Half position (半仓)Half the capital is in the market
20% — Light position (轻仓)Only a small portion in the market
0% — No position (空仓)No holdings, all capital waiting for opportunities
💡 Key insight: The higher the position, the more sensitive the account is to market movements. The lower the position, the less sensitive. There is no absolutely "correct" position — only the one most suited to the current market and your risk tolerance. (仓位越高，账户对市场波动的敏感度越高。没有绝对正确的仓位，只有最适合当下市场和你的风险承受能力的仓位！)

Position sizing is the first line of defense in risk management! (仓位是风险管理的第一道防线！)

2How Position Affects Returns

Same market move, different positions — completely different results. (同样的行情，不同仓位，收益完全不同。)

upsideExample: market rises 10%, account 1,000,000 (标的上涨10%)

PositionCapital deployedMarket moveAccount P&LAccount return
100% (满仓)1,000,000+10% +100,000 +10%
50% (半仓)500,000+10% +50,000 +5%
20% (轻仓)200,000+10% +20,000 +2%
Key insight: Market moves are given by the market — how much you earn is determined by your position. Bigger position = bigger returns; smaller position = smaller returns. (标的涨跌是市场给的，你赚多少，是仓位决定的。仓位越大，收益越大；仓位越小，收益越小。)

Tip: When uncertain, rather earn less than take heavy risks. Control position first, then control account volatility. Position management is the key to long-term survival. (不确定时，宁可少赚，也不要重仓冒险。先控制仓位，才能控制账户的波动。)

3How Position Affects Losses

Losses are also amplified by position — so control your position! (亏损也会被仓位放大，所以要控制仓位！)

downsideExample: market drops 10%, account 1,000,000 (标的下跌10%)

PositionCapital deployedMarket moveAccount lossLoss rate
100% (满仓)1,000,000−10% −100,000 −10%
50% (半仓)500,000−10% −50,000 −5%
20% (轻仓)200,000−10% −20,000 −2%

High position = big bucket (高仓位 = 桶大)

When markets fall, more water (losses) pours in. Bigger position → bigger damage.

Low position = small bucket (低仓位 = 桶小)

When markets fall, less damage. Smaller position → more controllable losses.

💡 Position doesn't change the market's direction — but it changes how much the loss hurts you. Controlling position = controlling drawdown = protecting your account. (仓位不会改变市场的方向，但会改变亏损对你的伤害。控制仓位，就是控制回撤，就是保护你的账户！)

Ultimate reminder (终极提醒)

Core goal of position management: Keep account volatility within tolerable range. Preserve ammunition for the next opportunity. Quantitative investing is not about who bets the most — it's about who survives the longest, stays the steadiest, and earns the most consistently! (量化投资不是比谁押得多，而是比谁活得久、活得稳、赚得久！)

4Fixed Position — Simple, Stable, Executable

Use a fixed ratio of capital for every trade. Suitable for most ordinary investors. (每次使用固定比例的资金进行交易。简单、好用、易坚持！)

methodFixed position = constant ratio of equity deployed

Fixed position (固定仓位): each time you invest a fixed percentage of total account equity. For example: always deploy 30%.

How it works: 30% fixed position example

StateEquityDeployed (30%)Reserve (70%)
Initial (初始)1,000,000300,000700,000
After +10% profit1,100,000330,000770,000
After −10% loss900,000270,000630,000
After another +10%990,000297,000693,000

Note how the deployed amount naturally adjusts with equity — it grows when you profit and shrinks when you lose. The ratio stays at 30%.

Advantages of fixed position (固定仓位的优点)

Simple & intuitive (简单易懂)

Just set a ratio — execution is straightforward.

Easy to backtest (易于回测)

Clear, unified rules make backtests more reliable.

Stable risk exposure (风险暴露稳定)

Risk level stays relatively consistent through ups and downs.

Easy to stick with (容易坚持)

No need for frequent judgment calls — long-term execution is easier.

Reference position ranges (常见比例参考)

StyleRatioCharacteristics
Conservative (保守型)10%–20%Low risk, capital preservation focused
Balanced (稳健型)20%–40%Moderate risk, balanced growth
Aggressive (积极型)40%–70%Higher risk, higher potential return

No absolute standard — what fits your risk tolerance is what matters most!

💡 Fixed position doesn't guarantee profits — it controls risk. Strategy P&L comes from the strategy itself; position only amplifies or dampens the result. Fixed position = simple + stable + executable — a good method for long-term survival! (固定仓位 = 简单 + 稳定 + 可执行，是长期生存的好方法！)

5Dynamic Position — Risk-Responsive Allocation

When the market is more uncertain, be more conservative. When it's calmer, be more aggressive. (市场越不确定，越要保守；市场越平稳，可以更积极！)

methodCore idea: adjust position based on market risk state

Dynamic position (动态仓位) adjusts the deployed ratio based on current market conditions. Higher volatility → lower position; lower volatility → position can be increased. (波动率越大，仓位越小；波动率越小，仓位可适当增大。)

Reference rule: volatility → position mapping (波动率与推荐仓位关系)

Annualized volatility (V)Recommended position (示例)
V > 40%10%–20% (very defensive)
20% < V ≤ 40%20%–40%
10% < V ≤ 20%40%–60%
V ≤ 10%60%–80% (can be more aggressive)

Low-volatility environment (低波动环境)

  • Annualized volatility: 12%
  • Recommended position: ~50%
  • Market is calm → increase position to capture opportunities

High-volatility environment (高波动环境)

  • Annualized volatility: 35%
  • Recommended position: ~20%
  • Market is turbulent → reduce position to protect capital

Advantages of dynamic position (动态仓位的优点)

  • Better drawdown control — reduce exposure when volatility is high (更好地控制回撤)
  • Improved capital efficiency — deploy more when conditions are favorable (提高资金使用效率)
  • Adapts to different market environments — more flexible and robust (适应不同市场环境)
  • Longer survival — risk control enables longer-term participation (长期生存能力更强)
💡 Dynamic position is not about predicting the market — it's a dynamic response to risk! Control risk first, then stay in the game long-term. (动态仓位不是预测市场，而是对风险的动态响应！先控制风险，才能在长期留下来。)

6Leverage & Alpha — Leverage Doesn't Create Skill

Leverage doesn't create excess returns — it only amplifies what already exists. (杠杆不会创造超额收益，它只是放大已有的收益和风险！)

dangerLeverage amplifies results, not ability

Raising leverage or position size only amplifies both returns AND risk simultaneously — it doesn't make the strategy itself any better. (提高杠杆或仓位，只会同时放大收益和风险，并不能让策略本身变得更优秀。)

Same strategy, different leverage (同一策略，不同杠杆)

Metric1× (no leverage)2× (or 2× position)3× (or 3× position)
Annual return (年化收益率)15%30%45%
Volatility (波动率)10%20%30%
Sharpe ratio1.51.51.5
Leverage ↑→ Return ↑+ Volatility ↑= Sharpe unchanged (夏普比率不变)

The strategy itself hasn't improved — you've just taken on more risk exposure. (策略本身并没有变好！只是承担了更大的风险敞口。)

  • Short-term may earn more — long-term may die faster. (短期可能赚得多，长期可能死得快。)
  • Leverage amplifies emotional volatility, increasing decision errors. (杠杆会放大情绪波动，增加决策失误。)
  • Once a black swan hits, you may be instantly back to square one. (一旦遇到黑天鹅，可能瞬间回到起跑线。)

Where does real Alpha come from? (Alpha 来自哪里？)

✗ Wrong approach: more leverage

  • Double the position (40% → 80%)
  • Double the leverage (1× → 2×)
  • Result: returns and risks both double — may earn more, may lose faster!

✓ Right approach: improve the strategy

  • Improve win rate — make the strategy more likely to win
  • Improve P/L ratio — earn more when right, lose less when wrong
  • Reduce drawdown / volatility — make the strategy more stable
  • Improve Sharpe ratio — more sustainable, more controllable

This is real Alpha! (这才是真正的 Alpha！)

★Leverage is just a multiplier — it doesn't create excess returns. Get it right first, then scale up! (杠杆只是倍增器，不会创造超额收益。先做对，再做大！) To earn higher returns, improve the strategy's quality — don't blindly add leverage. (想要更高收益，应该提升策略质量，而不是盲目加杠杆！)
