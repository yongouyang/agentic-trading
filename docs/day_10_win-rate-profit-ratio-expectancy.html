Win Rate, Profit/Loss Ratio & Expected Return

A high win rate does not mean you're making money! (胜率高 ≠ 一定赚钱！) The real question is: does the strategy have a mathematical foundation for long-term profitability? (一个策略到底有没有长期赚钱的数学基础？)

★Overview — The Three Pillars of Strategy Evaluation

Today you will learn: Win Rate, Profit/Loss Ratio, Expected Return, and why low-win-rate strategies can still be profitable.

frequency

Win Rate (胜率)

How many trades won? Measures frequency, not magnitude.

quality

Profit/Loss Ratio (盈亏比)

How much bigger is a win vs a loss? Measures quality of each trade.

verdict

Expected Return (期望收益)

Is the strategy worth repeating long-term? The ultimate verdict.

Core viewpoint: What truly matters is not winning every time, but earning enough when you win and losing little when you lose. (真正重要的不是每次都赢，而是赢的时候赚得够多，输的时候亏得够少。)

1Win Rate — How Often Do You Win?

Win rate answers one question: out of all trades, how many were winners? (胜率只回答一个问题：在所有交易中，赢了多少次？)

Win Rate (胜率)= Winning trades (盈利交易次数)÷ Total trades (总交易次数)× 100%

Why win rate alone is misleading (为什么胜率不代表一定盈利)

  • Win rate: 60%
  • Average win: +5%
  • Average loss: −2%

Scenario B: higher win rate, but losing

💡 Key point: Win rate tells you "how often" you win — it measures frequency (数量), not quality (质量). Whether a strategy makes money also depends on how much each win earns vs how much each loss costs. Win rate is just the first step in evaluation — far from sufficient! (胜率是"数量"，盈利是"质量"。一个好的策略，赢的时候赚得多，输的时候亏得少。)

2Profit/Loss Ratio — Win Big, Lose Small?

The profit/loss ratio measures the "size gap" between a typical win and a typical loss. (盈亏比衡量的是：一次盈利和一次亏损的"大小差距"。)

Profit/Loss Ratio (盈亏比)= Average Win (平均盈利)÷ Average Loss (平均亏损) → Higher is better (>1 preferred)

Why the P/L ratio is critical (为什么盈亏比很重要)

Scenario A: high win rate, low P/L ratio

  • Win rate: 70%, P/L ratio: 0.5
  • Win +1 per trade, lose −2 per trade
  • 10 trades: 7×1 − 3×2 = +1 (barely profitable)

Scenario B: low win rate, high P/L ratio

  • Win rate: 40%, P/L ratio: 3.3
  • Win +8.25% per trade, lose −2.5% per trade
  • 10 trades: 4×8.25 − 6×2.5 = +18.0% (highly profitable)
💡 The P/L ratio determines whether one big win can cover multiple small losses. A strategy needs a sufficiently high P/L ratio so that the minority of winners covers the majority of losers. (盈亏比决定了：一次大赚，能否覆盖多次小亏。一个策略要长期赚钱，不能只看胜率有多高，更要看盈亏比是否足够大！)

3Expected Return — Is the Strategy Worth Doing Long-Term?

Expected return answers: if you repeat this strategy many times, will you make money on average? (期望收益衡量的是：长期重复后，这个策略是否值得做。)

Expected Return (期望收益)= Win Rate× Avg Win− Loss Rate× Avg Loss

where Loss Rate = 1 − Win Rate (失败率 = 1 − 胜率)

verdictThree possible outcomes (三种结果)

Positive expectancy (正期望)

Expected return > 0

  • Long-term, account will grow
  • Strategy is worth doing!

Zero expectancy (零期望)

Expected return = 0

  • Account stays flat long-term
  • Pointless — fees will eat you!

Negative expectancy (负期望)

Expected return < 0

  • Long-term, account will shrink
  • Fix the strategy or abandon it!

5Trend Strategies — Why Low Win Rate Still Wins

Trends persist. A few big moves generate outsized returns. Small losses are the cost of waiting for the big one. (趋势有延续性，少数行情能带来巨大收益！)

strategyCore logic of trend strategies (趋势策略的核心逻辑)

Small losses many times, big gains a few times — one big win covers all the small losses.
(小亏很多次，靠少数大行情赚大钱，一次大赚覆盖所有小亏！)

Typical characteristics (典型特征)

Low win rate (胜率通常不高)

Often small losses — typically 20%–40% win rate.

High P/L ratio (盈亏比很高)

Wins are much larger than losses.

Few big movers drive returns (靠少数大行情)

A handful of trend captures contribute the majority of profit.

How trend strategies capture big moves (趋势策略如何抓住大行情)

Follow the trend (顺势而为)

Don't predict tops — follow the trend and let profits run.

Let profits run (设置合理盈利目标)

Don't exit too early — let winners grow.

Strict stop-loss (严格止损)

Small losses cut quickly; control single-trade loss.

Patient waiting (耐心等待机会)

Trade less, but catch the big ones — quality over quantity.

★Trend strategies don't pursue high win rates — they pursue high P/L ratios and positive expected returns. Small losses are the cost; big wins are the harvest. As long as the big wins appear often enough, the strategy stays profitable long-term. (趋势策略不追求高胜率，而是追求高盈亏比和正期望。小亏是代价，大赚是收获，只要大赚出现的次数足够，策略就能长期盈利。)
