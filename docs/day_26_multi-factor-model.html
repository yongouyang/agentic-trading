Multi-Factor Model — Evaluate Stocks from Multiple Dimensions

Facing thousands of stocks, we can't look at just one indicator — score from multiple angles, combine the scores, and find the batch most worth investing in. Different factors reflect company value from different angles; combining factors improves stock-selection stability. (用多个维度评估股票，选出更好的投资标的。)

2What is a Factor? (什么是因子?)

A factor = a characteristic that can explain differences in stock returns (因子 = 能够解释股票收益差异的特征). It's the indicator we use to measure how a stock performs on some dimension.

Different factors describe a company from different angles; combining multiple factors evaluates stocks more comprehensively and more stably. Value finds "cheap" companies, Quality finds "excellent" companies, Momentum finds "strong" companies.

1. Value factors (价值因子)

Look for underpriced companies (寻找价格被低估的公司).

Why use multiple factors? (为什么要使用多个因子?)

More complete (更全面)

Cover different dimensions; information is more complete.

More robust (更稳健)

A single factor may fail; a combination is steadier.

Diversifies risk (分散风险)

Avoids the extreme risk of relying on one factor.

Complementary (互补增强)

Different factors can complement and strengthen each other.

Like a jigsaw puzzle — every piece matters, and only combined is the picture complete. (就像拼图，每一块都重要，组合起来才更完整！)

💡 Goal of processing: make every factor a comparable score (usually 0–100) with a consistent direction — higher is always better. (让所有因子都变成可比较的分数，并且方向一致。)

Why do we need a Factor Engine? (为什么需要 Factor Engine?)

  • Unified standard (统一标准): factors have different units and ranges; adding them raw would be distorted.
  • Unified direction (统一方向): some factors are better high, some better low — all must become "bigger is better".
  • Noise reduction (降低噪音): handle outliers and missing values, improving factor stability.
  • Comparability (可比较性): only stocks scored on the same ruler are meaningfully comparable.

Practice tips (实践小贴士)

  • Factor calculation frequency: usually monthly / quarterly.
  • Update frequency should match trading frequency.
  • Handle abnormal factor values promptly (e.g. clip extreme values).
  • Factors need regular backtesting to verify they still work.
  • Record factor history — avoid look-ahead (未来函数) problems!

4Why Can't Factors Be Added Directly? (为什么因子不能直接相加?)

Different units, different directions, different ranges — standardization is required first. (量纲不同、方向不同、范围不同，需要标准化处理。)

exampleOne stock, three raw factor values

💎 PE (lower better)🛡️ ROE (higher better)📈 Past-6M return (higher better)
Raw value1218%+32%
Range / unitusually 5 ~ 100+, no unitusually −20% ~ 30%+, percentageusually −50% ~ +100%+, percentage
DirectionNegative (越低越好)Positive (越高越好)Positive (越高越好)
Can we add them directly?✗ No! (不能!)
💡 Conclusion: direction inconsistent, units inconsistent, ranges inconsistent — you MUST standardize first! (方向不一致、量纲不一致、范围不一致，必须先标准化！)

Typical standardization pipeline (标准化处理的典型流程)

1. Outlier handling (异常值处理)
remove extremes, reduce interference from anomalous data
→
2. Unify direction (方向统一)
positive factors unchanged; negative factors inverted, e.g. PE → −PE
→
3. Standardize / normalize (标准化/归一化)
Z-Score: Z = (x−μ)/σ, or Min-Max: X′ = (x−min)/(max−min)
→
4. Rank / percentile (排名/分位数)
convert to percentile rank — more robust (更稳健)
→
5. Factor score (因子得分)
map to 0–100, bigger is better

💡 Only the resulting factor scores are comparable (可比性) and can be combined!

💡 Common practice: percentile-rank first, then map to 0–100. (先分位数排名，再映射到 0 ~ 100 分！)

Standardization key points (标准要点总结)

  • Factors come from different dimensions; raw values cannot be added directly.
  • Pipeline: outlier handling → direction unification → standardization → rank mapping.
  • Only the final 0–100 factor scores are comparable.
  • Standardization is the most critical — and most easily overlooked — step in a factor model!
📌 One line to remember: unprocessed factors ≠ usable factors; standardized factors = comparable factors. (不处理的因子 ≠ 因子；标准化的因子 = 可比较的因子。)

5Factor Model — Combining Factors (组合因子)

The Factor Model's job: combine multiple factor scores with sensible weights into a composite score (综合得分) for every stock.

exampleA common three-factor model

Value 价值 40%
Quality 质量 40%
Momentum 动量 20%

Value factor + Quality factor + Momentum factor = Composite score (0–100). Weights sum to 100% and can be tuned and optimized per strategy goals. (权重之和 = 100%，可根据策略目标进行调整和优化。)

Weights shape the composite score (权重影响综合得分)

  • Raise the value weight (e.g. 50% / 30% / 20%): high-value stocks rise more in the ranking; style tilts toward "low valuation" (低估值).
  • Raise the momentum weight (e.g. 30% / 40% / 30%): strong-momentum stocks more easily reach the front; style tilts toward "trend following" (趋势跟随).

Key points (关键要点)

  • Core of a Factor Model: sensible weighting + scientific combination (合理赋权 + 科学组合).
  • Weight settings significantly affect selection results and strategy style.
  • Weights are not fixed — they need periodic testing and optimization.
  • The composite score is only step one of selection; it doesn't directly determine position size.
  • Multi-factor combination reduces single-factor failure risk.

How are weights determined? (权重如何确定?)

  • 🎯 Rule of thumb (经验法则): set initial weights from your investment philosophy.
  • 📊 Historical backtest (历史回测): search for weight combinations with better historical performance.
  • 🤖 Machine learning (机器学习): let algorithms learn optimal weights automatically.
  • 🔄 Dynamic adjustment (动态调整): adapt weights to the market environment.

Tip (小贴士)

There is no absolutely correct answer for weights — fitting your own strategy goals and risk preference matters most. (权重没有绝对正确答案，适合自己的策略目标和风险偏好才是最重要的！)

Factor Model = factor combiner (因子组合器): it merges the signals of many factors into a more stable, more comprehensive basis for stock selection. Next: how high-scoring stocks are handed to the Portfolio for capital allocation. (把多个因子的信号整合起来，形成更稳定、更全面的选股依据！)

7Factor Model ≠ Portfolio (因子模型 ≠ Portfolio)

A high score does not mean a big position — different jobs, each doing its own duty. The factor model only "selects stocks more worth watching"; the real "how much to buy, how to allocate capital" is the Portfolio's work. (评分高不代表仓位高，职责不同，各司其职。)

Common mistakes (常见误区)

  • Mistake 1: higher score → heavier position.
    Correct: weigh risk, valuation, industry, and money management together. (要综合考虑风险、估值、行业和资金管理等多方面因素。)
  • Mistake 2: only look at factors, not the portfolio.
    Correct: portfolio-level risk exposure and correlation matter just as much. (组合层面的风险暴露和相关性同样重要。)
  • Mistake 3: never rebalance.
    Correct: as markets and holdings change, rebalance regularly to keep the portfolio healthy. (随着市场变化和持仓收益，定期再平衡保持组合健康。)

Practice tips (实践小贴士)

  • More factors is not better — effectiveness and stability are what count.
  • Validate factors regularly; promptly remove failed ones.
  • Watch correlations between factors — avoid double-counting.
  • Backtesting is only the start; live trading needs risk control and discipline.
  • Stay simple, interpretable, executable — that's how you win long-term.
